# Task 010 — Alternative research paths and objective-value audit

**Stage S1.** Work from the same approved research-plan commit as Task 009. Read all canonical planning files and prior reports #1–#16.

Aim: challenge whether a *single universal finite algebra* is even the best formulation of the intellectual goal, **without losing** the original aim of understanding the fundamental machinery of mathematical proof.

Compare at least:
- finite generator basis under constrained translations;
- an equivalence class / hierarchy of generating presentations;
- a universal property / free mathematical structure;
- a fibred/institutional family indexed by foundations;
- a higher-dimensional proof/computation object;
- a graph or multiway causal representation;
- an obstruction/classification programme without a universal object;
- a practical proof-operation interoperability/certificate layer as a separately labelled applied payoff.

For each option: mathematical object; existing theorems and closest prior art; what it preserves; what it cannot preserve; relationship to finite generation; definitional complexity; possible novel theorem; likely failure mode; bounded discriminating test; expected value to the north star. Add omitted alternatives.

Explicitly audit previous methodological errors: prematurely narrow source class, confuse proof identity and derivability, overfocus on computability, impose anti-interpreter criterion before structural comparison, and allow divergent agent conjectures.

**Deliver:** research/reviews/012-strategy-options.md as a PR with a comparative decision matrix and recommendations. This is a strategy audit, not a product/business-case pitch. Do not freeze a conjecture, rewrite the charter, or implement an algebra.
