**State:** Proposed bounded follow-up, not permission for broad proof search.

Read the Stage 0.7 reconciliation, PR #9, reports 006 and 007, and the project's research protocol. Do **not** attempt to prove existence of an as-yet-undefined H1.

Deliver (1) an explicit interface definition including source judgments, contexts, translations and reflection; and (2) at least two mathematically precise rival non-vacuity predicates N.

Each N must be run *unchanged* against: encoded checker, typed-path rule-table construction, universal finitely presented group, ordinary LF adequacy, and linear proof translation. For each produce a truth-value or a rigorous explanation of unknown. Give adversarial constructions trying to defeat N by refactoring/inlining.

Do not silently rule out universal algebraic representations merely because they are powerful; articulate what *proof-theoretic structure* an admissible encoding must expose. Do not use primitive count, runtime efficiency, syntactic naming, or a claim that a construction 'feels like an interpreter' as sole grounds.

Provide precise logical dependencies: which properties are proven, which are conjectured, and which require owner choice. The final deliverable is a **decision matrix** and one recommended candidate H1 or an explicit finding that none survived.

Do not implement a prover or change the original charter. Do not treat a restricted result as a bridge to universal existence without proving that bridge.
