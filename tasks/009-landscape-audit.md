**Stage S1.** Read RESEARCH_PLAN.md, LANDSCAPE.md, COMPARISON_RUBRIC.md, DECISIONS.md, OPERATING_PROTOCOL.md and relevant prior reports. Do not design a new algebra or prove H1.

Aim: identify the broadest credible landscape of mature mathematical structures/theories relevant to the generation, justification, composition, equivalence and cross-foundation translation of proof operations.

1. Audit the seed LANDSCAPE; add **missing families**, not just papers within listed families.
2. For each family recover an exact relevant concept/theorem, a limit/counterexample, relationships to neighboring families and whether a deep dive could materially change our framing.
3. Distinguish direct prior art, useful adjacent structures, methodological analogies and speculative connections. In particular evaluate clones/Lawvere; structural/categorical proof theory; harmony; HoTT/cubical; polygraphs; general logics/institutions; operads/PROPs; concurrency; rewriting; automated deduction; Wolfram multiway; and Langlands as an analogy.
4. Produce a coverage matrix with 'investigated / partial / unknown / deferred' evidence and priority justified by decision value, not familiarity.
5. Red-team the current north-star formulation: identify overlooked alternative objectives, false assumptions, and directions likely not worth pursuing.
6. Propose a prioritized short list for deep dives with why each is *not already answered* by the older reports.

**Deliver:** research/reviews/011-landscape-audit.md as a PR. Propose changes to LANDSCAPE separately or in reviewed diff; don't silently declare the seed map complete. Source precise primary passages where decisive. No giant unannotated bibliography; no implementation.
