# Task 001: adversarial existence and novelty audit

**Review requested; Stage 0 remains closed to implementation.** Audit date: 2026-10-08. Repository baseline: `e3d2e6e032283d2d08aaa186501ba650e82a0cdf`. All Stage 0 documents and the assignment were read in full before changes. This report changes no charter or definitions.

## Executive verdict

**Recommendation: NARROW the mathematical question; STOP investment in a new universal algebra or prover pending independent review.** [INFERENCE] The current conjecture is not a statement whose existence or novelty can be settled: its class, environment, equality, and fidelity conditions are unspecified. Under permissive readings it admits an interpreter construction; substantial non-vacuous representation and composition are already provided by logical frameworks. Under several stronger readings there are concrete obstructions. No genuinely novel theorem or independently verified open problem has been established here.

The most consequential findings are:

- **Existing structural adequacy is stronger than “proof checking.”** LF gives compositional bijections between particular source proofs and canonical LF objects [B1]. Dedukti has preservation and conservativity for functional pure type systems, without requiring source normalization [B5]. Neither is a theorem about every effectively described proof system with arbitrary proof congruence.
- **Finite framework machinery and fixed inference content differ.** LF represents object rules by signature constants; rewriting logic represents them by rewrite rules. Those are legitimate framework presentations, but do not certify that each object rule is derived in an unchanged logical core [B1–B5]. The charter explicitly permits *marked* extra trust, not silently treating it as justified mathematics.
- **An unrestricted shared structural theory can destroy fidelity.** The direct linear-to-cartesian reading fails on duplication. A cartesian closed category with a dualizing object is a preorder [B9], so cannot faithfully retain distinct parallel proofs. These are conditional refutations, not impossibility theorems for tagged or many-sorted frameworks.
- **Cyclic-to-inductive conservativity is actually false in a restricted language.** Berardi–Tatsuta's 2-Hydra formula is cyclically provable but unprovable in the specified explicit-induction system, even with the stated successor axioms [B11]. A translation that silently adds arithmetic or inductive predicates has changed its obligations.
- **Local validity and costs require separate claims.** Global trace conditions are decidable on finite cyclic representations [B10]; this does not make local inference validity sufficient. Resolution has polynomial DAG refutations for families needing exponential tree-like refutations [B12]. Neither observation rules out finite signatures.

**Strongest counterargument to this verdict:** a carefully specified framework could preserve resources, equality, and certified global annotations together. None of the conditional failures rules that out. **Response:** that is a defensible question only after fixing those annotations, extension rules, and equality; it is not evidence of novelty. The single next gate below tests precisely the gap between LF's raw-proof adequacy and proof identity modulo permutations.

### Two rigorously different target versions

These are proposals, not amendments. They make tradeoffs visible rather than repair the charter implicitly.

**V-A: declared-extension structural adequacy (baseline).** [CONJECTURE as a class-wide statement; VERIFIED_SOURCE for instances in B1/B5.] Fix one finite typed framework calculus K including its typing and conversion rules. Independently define C_A as finitely presented, finitary natural-deduction calculi with finite judgment sorts, explicitly scoped binders, and effective syntactic rule side conditions. A source presentation includes a declared raw-proof α-congruence, not arbitrary semantic proof identity. Require a uniform effective procedure producing a finite signature Σ_S and translations T_S, F_S such that:

1. For every valid source environment E and judgment J, source derivability holds iff K has a witness of T_S(J) in T_S(E), Σ_S.
2. Source raw proofs of J, modulo α, are in bijection with a specified canonical class of target witnesses modulo K conversion. The canonical class and adequacy must be justified independently of a generic `check(S,d,J)` predicate.
3. For every well-typed object/proof substitution σ, `F_S(d[σ]) =_K F_S(d)[F_S(σ)]`; corresponding grafting/discharge operations commute.
4. Every Σ_S declaration representing a source inference rule is listed as **additional source trust**, or supplied as a derived macro with a K derivation. No logical soundness of the source is inferred from adequacy.

No requirement equates source normalization or permutation congruence with K equality; no causal or cost promise. Natural deduction is independently motivated, but “effective side conditions” remains broad: some members may require explicit syntax and a check relation. Reject those encodings under item 2 rather than assert LF covers the entire class. **Tradeoff:** recognizable framework mathematics; strong known instances; finite machinery with variable inference content. This cannot be advertised as a new foundation-independent derivation of every rule.

**V-B: fixed-core quotient and resource fidelity.** [CONJECTURE; existence and novelty UNKNOWN.] Quantify over an independently fixed test class C_B: intuitionistic propositional natural deduction with products/implication, unit-free multiplicative linear logic (MLL) sequents, the dependent λΠ typing calculus, and regular CLKIDω proofs with the trace condition of B10. Fix these presentations and source congruences *before* constructing a translation: βη for the first, equality of occurrence-labeled cut-free MLL nets for the second, αβ conversion of typed objects for the third, and isomorphism of rooted rule/trace-labeled cyclic graphs for the fourth. Where operations do not preserve the specified domain (e.g. MLL cut-free composition), supply the chosen cut-elimination/normalization operation and prove it well-defined, rather than silently grafting.

Require one finite core A with a fixed effective typing relation and fixed congruence; uniform effective T_S,F_S; derivability preservation/reflection over **every target inhabitant of translated judgments**; and `d ~_S e iff F_S(d) =_A F_S(e)`. Specify commuting composition and dependent substitution on their respective domains. Resource occurrence maps must preserve and reflect duplication, erasure, and context splitting. For cyclic objects, accepted images must preserve/reflect global validity, with explicit certificates whose checking is derived in A. Causal claims are limited to mapped surviving inference occurrences, not execution scheduling or all normalization histories.

Environments may add object symbols, scoped hypotheses, mathematical axioms, and terminating definitional abbreviations. They may **not** add new primitive inference constants or change A's conversion relation; object inference steps must be derived macros. Imported axioms remain assumptions with provenance. Inspect the transitive dependencies of every translated rule for this condition. Definitions that return `Proof(J)` on a checker boolean fail the nontriviality conditions below.

**Tradeoff:** substantially stronger than the inspected general framework theorems; it may be impossible or require relaxing a named condition. The heterogeneous class does not require that different foundations share identical connectives or equality. Its finite member list is a stress test, not universality over all mathematics. Global validity and quotient equality may make effective checking difficult; decidable proof equality is deliberately *not* required. No theorem in this audit establishes V-B. A claim about a larger class needs a separately motivated closure principle, not inclusion by successful encoding.

## 3. Interpreter benchmark and a nontriviality filter

### Explicit vacuous construction

[INFERENCE: informal construction, not implemented.] Let S supply a total decidable certificate checker `c_S(E,d,J)∈{0,1}`. Fix a finite-register/finite-alphabet universal machine U, with fixed finite transition schemas, list/string constructors, and a rule accepting a finite halted run whose output is 1. Put the program code of c_S, and the codes of E,d,J, in data. Define target “proof of encoded J under S,E” to be a run of U on those codes that accepts. Enumerating such finite runs is effective. Then:

`∃d. c_S(E,d,J)=1  iff  ∃d,τ. τ is an accepting U-run on (code(c_S),E,d,J)`.

The forward implication uses totality and supplies the actual run; the reverse reads its accepted output. If checking is only partial but accepts exactly valid certificates, accepting runs still capture the r.e. derivability set. A primitive `accept(c_S,E,d,J)` defined to mean the check succeeds makes the same point even more directly, while adding arbitrary trusted computation in a single operation.

This is R0/R1-relative universality, not R2–R4. Encode source grafting and substitutions as data transformations and it can *claim* syntactic composition too; computational simulation does not establish the target's proof composition equals source composition. Different U runs/steps are machine events, not source inferences. A program containing all source rules has variable inference content despite a fixed instruction set. Rewriting logic's reflection discussion (§7 of B3) is an existing sophisticated relative-universality baseline; its quoted finite universal theory is attributed there to Clavel–Meseguer. That original reflection theorem was not independently retrieved here, so this report does not promote its full hypotheses to VERIFIED_SOURCE.

### Checkable restriction for submitted encodings

[CONJECTURE: proposed acceptance discipline; not a universal semantic characterization.] Require the following *together*, with finite auditable translation artifacts:

1. Each source judgment translates to an exposed typed judgment on its translated **context boundary**, not a proposition asserting acceptance of a source program or of a whole proof code. Proof witnesses occur as inhabitants, not arguments of `check`. Arbitrary object data may be coded; adequacy must explain which data positions are syntax and which are evidence.
2. For each source rule schema r, submit a fixed target term/diagram template M_r with named typed premise holes, binder scopes, and a boundary occurrence-use map. Translation is a homomorphism: `F(r(d1,...,dk))=M_r[F(d1),...,F(dk)]`. No template inspects the code of a premise derivation or passes it to evaluation. Normalize acyclic transparent abbreviations and audit this property on the expanded templates.
3. For a linear multiplicative rule, the premise holes/occurrences are used once with the prescribed split; erasure/duplication elsewhere is explicitly attributed to the source rule or modality. Substitution/grafting equations and source identity relations have target derivations; for R3 require reflection, not merely such equations.
4. Every template head is either a fixed core constructor, a certified derived macro, or an **explicitly listed primitive extension**. An unproved function from premise-proof types to conclusion-proof types is classified as an object rule, irrespective of being declared “axiom” or “environment parameter.” Strict V-B rejects the last category; V-A reports it.
5. Target equality used for R3 is its declared congruence. A type `EqSource(d,e)` is evidence *about* equality; it is not the equality of F(d) and F(e). Do not restrict reflection by rechecking target witnesses with c_S.

The U construction fails items 1–2: its conclusion asserts acceptance and its witness traverses derivation codes. LF judgments-as-types encodings with rule constants can pass 1–2, including higher-order premise holes; ordinary adequate encodings are not rejected simply for using LF. They pass item 4 only with the correct extension classification. λΠ modulo encodings require explicit rewrite/typing audit, not a ban by name.

**Unresolved loophole:** a representation can decorate a interpreter with structural-looking wrappers, or hide evaluation inside a purported derived macro. The finite syntactic inspection rejects the exhibited interpreter, but no proved invariant here excludes *every* disguised interpreter. For candidate review, inspection must reach definitions and evidence dependencies; source-specific computation used for rule side conditions must have separate bounded evidence. Proving that this filter is both non-vacuous and broad enough is itself a gate obligation. Polynomial overhead or compositionality alone does not remove vacuity.

Even banning a primitive named “interpreter” does not prevent composition of universal machine instructions. **Counterargument:** compositional structural maps are stronger. **Follow-up:** inspect the templates, boundaries, and equality under the proposed filter; do not retroactively weaken it to admit a preferred encoding.

### F03 — Hidden inference rules

**Claim tested:** logical framework presentations derive source inference content solely from an unchanged core plus innocuous environment data.

**Evidence:** B1 Σ_FOL includes inference constants, B3 adds rules for T, B5 Definition 21 adds S-indexed universes/product codes and decoding rewrites; B6 gives actual metatheory obligations. **Concrete test:** extend a well-formed LF signature with `bad : true(P)` for an otherwise unprovable object proposition P. Type-checking `bad` verifies a proof **relative to that signature**, not derivability of P in the unextended source. Similarly a well-typed constant `r : true(P) → true(Q)` is a primitive object inference until derived or trusted explicitly. **Status/result:** VERIFIED_SOURCE + INFERENCE example; fatal to the “no extra source trust” interpretation of those presentations, not to their relative adequacy. **Follow-up:** count constants by logical role, report dependencies, and require conservativity/certification for claimed eliminable extensions.

### F04 — Incompatible proof identities

**Claim tested:** one cartesian closed proof category with classical negation implemented by an involutive canonical double-negation isomorphism faithfully preserves a source with distinct parallel proofs.

**Evidence:** B9 Proposition 1. **Concrete witnesses:** in positive intuitionistic product syntax, the two projections `π1,π2 : p×p→p` differ (interpret p as a two-element set). In a preorder their images coincide. Faithfulness therefore fails. **Status/result:** VERIFIED_SOURCE theorem + INFERENCE application; fatal to this shared categorical structure. **Counterargument:** separate linear/unrestricted modes, continuation encodings, or different connectives escape the assumptions. They need new preservation statements rather than a claim of direct connective preservation.

**Independent effective-equality obstruction:** [INFERENCE] define objects X_e,Y_e and two raw arrows a_e,b_e:X_e→Y_e for each natural e, with identities and no composable nonidentity arrows. Raw validity is decidable by two schematic arrow constructors. Set `a_e ~ b_e` iff machine e halts on empty input; no other distinct arrows are equal. This is an r.e. congruence: a finite halting run certifies a generating equality, and only identities can compose with it. A uniform computable faithful translation into a target with **decidable equality** would decide halting by comparing F(a_e),F(b_e). Thus finite certificate checking alone cannot justify universally decidable faithful proof equality. This synthetic system is not claimed to be an ordinary mathematical foundation; it is a negative control for the charter's broad effective class. If C requires decidable source congruence, it is excluded openly. **Follow-up:** declare equality effectiveness separately; V-B does not assume its decidability.

### F05 — False graph locality

**Claim tested:** ordinary local inference validity plus well-matched cyclic back-links is sufficient for sound proofhood.

**Evidence:** B10 Definitions 5.4–5.5/7.3, Proposition 7.4; B11 Theorem 8.3 strengthens the cross-style objection. **Concrete invalid pre-proof:** a contraction node with conclusion `P ⊢ Q` and premise `P,P ⊢ Q`, a weakening node returning to `P ⊢ Q`, and a back-link to the first node. Both classical structural inferences are locally valid; without any unfolding there is no infinitely progressing trace. Interpret independent atoms with P true, Q false: the conclusion is invalid. **Status/result:** INFERENCE example grounded in VERIFIED_SOURCE criterion; fatal to bare local sufficiency. **Counterargument:** certificate annotations can enforce global validity, and finite checking remains possible; B13 supplies a genuine labelled-fragment alternative. **Follow-up:** fix annotations and their completeness claims; no impossibility of local certification or finite signatures is asserted.

### F06 — False order invariance

**Claim tested:** operations appearing reorderable can always be commuted without changing results/identity, or any construction history has canonical partial-order semantics.

**Concrete test:** the finite rewrite presentation `a→b`, `a→c` with distinct irreducible b,c gives two incompatible reductions from a. They are alternatives in conflict, not independent events. If an alleged causal semantics forgets the choice and identifies all “orders from a,” it is incorrect. Disjoint redexes in a pure first-order term can commute; overlaps, deletion, and duplication require residual analysis. **Status/result:** INFERENCE; fatal to the blanket reading, not a counterexample to a correctly defined independence relation. B8 occurrence distinctions/weight dependencies demonstrate why unlabeled adjacency is insufficient. **Follow-up:** require a diamond/residual law for *declared* independent events and separate conflict. Scheduling a proof checker never supplies this law.

### F07 — Infinite/effective boundary

**Claim tested:** all mathematically meaningful valid reasoning has complete finite effectively accepted certificates.

**Evidence:** B10 Theorem 5.9 and discussion after it: recursive infinite trees suffice for standard completeness, but standard validity is non-arithmetic and admits no effectively enumerated complete proof subclass. **Additional boundary argument:** the ω-rule has infinitely many premises `{Γ⊢φ(n): n∈N}` and conclusion `Γ⊢∀n φ(n)`. A finite node is not a literal proof of all premises; a finite program enumerating premises is not a certificate of its total correctness without further evidence. Any effective finite checker yields an r.e. set of conclusions by enumerating strings, so cannot be complete for a non-r.e. semantic consequence relation (including full standard second-order validity, using categorical second-order arithmetic). **Status/result:** VERIFIED_SOURCE for the FOLID boundary; INFERENCE for the ω-rule/second-order observations. No separate primary second-order completeness theorem was recovered. Requires scope restriction; not fatal to the charter's current finite effective certificates. **Follow-up:** list excluded proof forms explicitly; do not advertise “all mathematics.”

### F08 — Minimality ambiguity

**Claim tested:** number of fundamental operation names identifies a unique meaningful minimal basis.

**Evidence:** B7's chosen finite CCC presentation, B1's typed λ presentation; interpreter §3. **Concrete test:** package k operators op_i as `apply(tag_i,args)` with finite list/tag constructors. The apparent operation count changes; the information and semantics of op_i have moved into a dispatcher. Conversely expand one macro into a long composition and the basis grows without changing expressibility. Even for λ syntax, replacing abstraction by typed combinator schemas changes binder treatment and costs. **Status/result:** INFERENCE; fatal to presentation-independent minimality without a metric. **Counterargument:** restrict definitional equivalence and allowable macro elimination. **Follow-up:** count total typing/equality/dispatch specification, arities and translation overhead. No minimality conjecture should pass Stage 0 at present.

### F09 — False efficiency equivalence

**Claim tested:** elimination of derived operations/sharing preserves proof length or search/checking cost up to a small uniform bound.

**Evidence:** B12 Theorems 4.1–4.2/Corollary 4.3. A DAG resolution proof can reuse a line; tree-like resolution must rederive it. The cited family has polynomial DAG and exponential tree refutations. **Status/result:** VERIFIED_SOURCE; fatal to a polynomial DAG-to-tree resolution claim on that family. A richer target retaining explicit sharing escapes it. An additional elementary example is repeated squaring: `u0=f; u_{i+1}=λx.u_i(u_i x)` has a shared description O(n) but the expanded β-normal form applies f exactly 2^n times. [INFERENCE] This demonstrates expansion of that proof/term, not that every equivalent proof is exponentially large. Exact general cut-elimination lower bounds were searched but not recovered from primary theorem text; no Statman theorem is asserted here. **Follow-up:** preserve DAG sharing as a separate choice; do not infer cheap normalization, checking or search from finitely many operations.

### F10 — Weak universal quantifier

**Claim tested:** “there exists a nontrivial C” establishes cross-foundation universality.

**Concrete test:** choose C={the positive intuitionistic calculus generated by one graph}; B7 already supplies its algebraic syntax. Or define C as systems admitting embeddings into the proposed A: universality becomes a restatement of membership. **Status/result:** VERIFIED_SOURCE baseline + INFERENCE diagnosis; fatal to the claimed force of an unrestricted existential C. V-B chooses test members independently but remains a finite comparative test. **Counterargument:** a mathematically motivated grammar of systems could define a broader class. **Follow-up:** publish that grammar and negative controls before establishing representations.

### Exact cyclic counterexample to carry forward

[VERIFIED_SOURCE: B11 Definition 3.5 and Theorem 8.3.] With `1=s0`, bounded quantifiers ranging over N, write:

- `Ha = ∀x∈N. p(0,0) ∧ p(1,0) ∧ p(x,1)`;
- `Hb = ∀x,y∈N. p(x,y) → p(sx,ssy)`;
- `Hc = ∀y∈N. p(sy,y) → p(0,ssy)`;
- `Hd = ∀x∈N. p(sx,x) → p(ssx,0)`;
- `H = (Ha ∧ Hb ∧ Hc ∧ Hd) → ∀x,y∈N. p(x,y)`.

In Σ_N={0,s,p,N} with N's usual two production schemas, H is CLKIDω-provable but is not provable in LKID even with `∀x∈N. sx≠0` and `∀x,y∈N. sx=sy→x=y`. The published argument supplies a Henkin countermodel, not a machine certificate in this audit. **Strongest objection:** the result is tied to this restricted language; stronger arithmetic environments can derive more. Precisely so: the scope/extension is part of the theorem and may not be omitted. This does not disprove a common meta-framework representing both systems separately.

Open objections requiring reviewer attention:

1. **Nontriviality is not yet a theorem.** The filter excludes the exhibited interpreter but may exclude legitimate side-condition-heavy presentations, or admit disguised evaluation. Uniform effective templates and occurrence maps need a precise admissible language.
2. **V-B's macro-only environment may be too severe.** Logical frameworks deliberately distinguish primitive object rules from mathematical assumptions; refusing them may erase the benefit of foundation independence. If that restriction is relaxed, acknowledge the return toward known framework universality.
3. **Quotient fidelity needs its own theorem.** Adequacy of raw proofs does not settle equality modulo reduction/permutation. Source congruences and target equality have to be fixed, not encoded as arbitrary added equations after inspection.
4. **A partial order may be inadequate for branch choice or cycles.** Independence/conflict and unfolding traces carry information absent from a plain DAG order. R4 cannot be read uniformly as chronology.
5. **Prior-art completeness is limited.** Deep inference, linear/dependent logical frameworks, polygraphs/coherence, foundational proof certificates, and p-optimal proof systems were leads, not full audited theorem families here. No universal negative theorem or novel surviving theorem is claimed from their absence. In particular, categorical and rewrite coherence results could subsume a proposed quotient extension.
6. **Evidence remains literary/informal.** Reviewer verification of the decisive primary passages and informal witnesses is still needed. Agreement of one researcher with a source is not FORMALIZED status.

## 7. Decision and single next gate

**Decision: NARROW; no promotion to implementation or operation enumeration.** [INFERENCE] There is enough adverse evidence to stop any claim that the original existential wording itself defines a promising new project. The only defensible remaining investment is a bounded theorem comparison, subject to external acceptance of this audit.

**Strongest surviving candidate:** V-B's fixed-core, declared-congruence, resource-sensitive embeddings for the independently chosen systems. [CONJECTURE, novelty UNKNOWN] Its apparent gap from prior art is simultaneous proof-identity/reflection and a strict extension boundary, not finite syntax or universal computation. It may collapse to an existing coherence/adequacy theorem or fail. Calling it a new conjecture of mathematical interest would be premature.

**Single discriminating next test: MLL permutation identity versus existing LF equality.** Use existing framework mathematics, first on paper; no custom implementation is needed. Fix unit-free cut-free classical MLL with atomic identity links and occurrence-labeled formulas. Declare source equality to be equality of nets, not raw rule trees. Fix the standard explicit sequent-rule LF signature, whose object proof constructors are uninterpreted constants and whose equality is LF conversion (state β or βη explicitly).

Starting with atomic axioms for p, r, q, form `D` with conclusions `p⊥,r⊥,p⊗r` by tensoring the p and r axioms. Compare:

1. Apply par to the two negative context occurrences of D, then tensor `p⊗r` with q's axiom.
2. Tensor D with q's axiom first, then apply par to those same negative context occurrences.

Both end at `⊢ p⊥⅋r⊥, (p⊗r)⊗q, q⊥`. They have the same occurrence-labeled net (three axiom links, two tensor links, one par link), but standard LF encodings have respectively outer tensor and outer par proof constants. Their canonical forms differ; βη does not commute uninterpreted constants. [INFERENCE: informal witness, not a run of LF.] Thus raw-tree adequacy alone does not preserve the declared net equality.

The next task must have exactly one outcome: **a primary-source-backed fully faithful representation theorem for this quotient in an existing framework under the stated trust boundary, or a precise obstruction to that proposed representation.** Include the positive pair above and the negative control `p⊢p⊗p` (linear formulation) to prevent “solve equality” by admitting cartesian duplication or identifying everything. Any added target rewrite equation must have its confluence/typing/faithfulness and trust consequences reported; an encoded equality predicate does not pass. A net-based canonical translation may be a valid alternative, but its substitution/composition compatibility must be demonstrated.

If a known theorem meets the test, record redundancy and stop claiming a new algebra is needed. If the standard encoding fails and another has not been justified, **do not infer novelty or impossibility for all encodings**. Request an independent mathematician's review of the remaining claim and filter. Proceed only after that explicit Stage 0 gate; this PR does not approve it.

Minimal proposed definition amendments, **not applied**: replace the existential unspecified C by named members/closure criteria; split primitive object rules from definitions/axioms in E; write the two reflection statements separately (existence of inhabitants versus equality); give each source a fixed congruence and resource boundary; state whether global certificates are allowed; delete any inferred minimality/efficiency claim.
