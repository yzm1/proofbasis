## S1. Selinger, "Finite dimensional Hilbert spaces are complete for dagger compact closed categories"
- LMCS Vol. 8 (3:06) 2012, pp. 1–12. DOI printed: 10.2168/LMCS-8(3:06)2012. Extended abstract: QPL 2008, ENTCS 270(1):113–119, 2011 (as printed in footnote; NB the task said "QPL 2010" — the paper itself says QPL 2008 workshop, ENTCS 2011).
- URL retrieved: https://arxiv.org/pdf/1207.6972 (file is the LMCS journal-formatted version, 12 pp.). Status: VERIFIED_SOURCE.
- Thm 2.1 (citing Selinger 2007) VERBATIM: "A well-typed equation between morphisms in the language of dagger compact closed categories follows from the axioms of dagger compact closed categories if and only if it holds, up to graph isomorphism, in the graphical language."
- Thm 2.2 VERBATIM: "Let M, N : A → B be two terms in the language of dagger compact closed categories. Suppose that [[M]] = [[N]] for every possible interpretation (of object variables as spaces and morphism variables as linear maps) in finite dimensional Hilbert spaces. Then M = N holds in the graphical language (and therefore, holds in all dagger compact closed categories)."
- Lemma 4.1 (Relative completeness) VERBATIM: "Let M be a (simple closed dagger traced monoidal) diagram. Then there exists an interpretation [[−]]_M in finite dimensional Hilbert spaces, depending only on M, such that for all N, [[N]]_M = [[M]]_M holds if and only if N and M are isomorphic diagrams."
- Proof of Lemma 5.13 (p.10–11) VERBATIM: "What we must show is that for all f ≠ g : A → B in C_Σ, there exists some dagger compact closed functor F : C_Σ → FinHilb such that F(f) ≠ F(g). Equivalently, we have to show that there exists a faithful dagger compact closed functor F̄ : C_Σ → FinHilb^X into some discrete power FinHilb^X = Π_{i∈X} FinHilb".
- Classification: structure = dagger compact closed (and dagger traced SMC); identity = equality in the free dagger CCC = graph isomorphism of diagrams (coherence identity, no βη-type computation beyond that); result = COLLECTIVE completeness (class of all interpretations in ONE fixed category FinHilb), equivalently a faithful structure-preserving functor into a discrete POWER FinHilb^X. Lemma 4.1 gives a per-term single interpretation (Statman-style "relative completeness") but NOT one interpretation separating all pairs.
- Limitations stated in source (§6): (a) holds for any char-0 field with non-trivial involution; also Z[i]. (b) "Non-equational properties": completeness fails for implications; example ∀f,g (f†ff†f = g†gg†g ⇒ f†f = g†g) holds in FinHilb but not in all dagger compact closed categories (counterexample Z5). (c) Bounded dimension: false for dimension 2 (Paré: tr(AABBAB)=tr(AABABB) holds for all 2×2 matrices but not in graphical language); dimension 3 open "to the author".
- Relevance (INFERENCE): Model of the strongest kind of "proof-identity Kripke completeness" for a structure with NO closure-in-the-intuitionistic-sense, no weakening/contraction; the model is concrete/non-syntactic but completeness is collective over interpretations, and only for equations. Addresses F04 only for one structural discipline (compact closed = MLL with ⊗=⅋ identified, plus mix-like units), not uniformly.

## S2. Hasegawa, Hofmann, Plotkin, "Finite dimensional vector spaces are complete for traced symmetric monoidal categories"
- In: Pillars of Computer Science (Trakhtenbrot Festschrift), LNCS 4800, pp. 367–385, Springer 2008. DOI on homepage link: 10.1007/978-3-540-78127-1_20.
- URL retrieved: https://www.kurims.kyoto-u.ac.jp/~hassei/papers/trace-boazfest.pdf (author's copy with Springer pagination 367–385). Status: VERIFIED_SOURCE.
- Abstract VERBATIM: "We show that the category FinVect_k of finite dimensional vector spaces and linear maps over any field k is (collectively) complete for the traced symmetric monoidal category freely generated from a signature, provided that the field has characteristic 0; this means that for any two different arrows in the free traced category there always exists a strong traced functor into FinVect_k which distinguishes them."
  [NB: abstract says "over any field k ... provided ... characteristic 0".]
- Free object: Net_(S,F) = cyclic networks modulo isomorphism; Prop. 2: "Net(S,F) forms a traced strict symmetric monoidal category"; Prop. 3: "Mod((S, F), C) ≃ TrMon(Net(S,F), C)".
- Thm 3 VERBATIM: "If two networks over a given signature have equal value under all interpretations over fields of the form Q(λ1, ..., λn) then they are isomorphic."
- Thm 4 VERBATIM (p.383): "Let k be a field of characteristic 0. If two networks over a given signature have equal value under all interpretations over k then they are isomorphic."
- Thm 5 VERBATIM: "If two networks have equal value under all interpretations over all finite fields then they are isomorphic."
- Cor 5 VERBATIM (p.384): "Let k be a field of characteristic 0. If two bidirectional nets have equal value under all interpretations over k then they are isomorphic." (compact closed case via Int construction; Int Net is the free compact closed category.)
- Open problems stated (p.383) VERBATIM: "Perhaps there is a uniform bound on the dimensions of the vector spaces needed for completeness. Alternatively there may be a result similar to those of Statman ... there may even a be single interpretation such that another network is isomorphism to N iff it has the same value as N in that interpretation." Also "we do not know if the corresponding result is true for any field of positive characteristic."
- Intro (p.368) VERBATIM on SMCC: "Mac Lane conjectured that the category of vector spaces over a field is complete for the symmetric monoidal closed category freely generated by a set of atoms. This was proved in a more general form by Soloviev [12]" — [12] = Soloviev, "Proof of a conjecture of S. Mac Lane", APAL 90, 101–162 (1997). (SECONDARY_ONLY for Soloviev's statement.)
- Classification: structure = traced SMC (and compact closed); identity = network isomorphism (free-structure equality); COLLECTIVE completeness over all strong traced functors to a fixed category FinVect_k; not a single faithful functor.
- Relevance (INFERENCE): a "concrete-model" completeness for proof identity of a resource-sensitive, cyclic structure; again specific to one structure; trivial cycles counted by dimension (shows identity must track "loops").

## S3. Simpson, "Categorical completeness results for the simply-typed lambda-calculus" (TLCA 1995, LNCS 902, pp. 414–427)
- Full paper: NOT_ACCESSED (no open copy found; searched research.ed.ac.uk / CiteSeerX via web search). Biblio (pages 414–427) confirmed in HHP ref [10] (VERIFIED in HHP text).
- Author's own announcement retrieved: categories mailing list, 4 Jul 1994, "Categorical completeness results for the simply-typed lambda calculus", URLs https://mta.ca/~cat-dist/archive/1994/94-7 and https://mta.ca/~cat-dist/catlist/1999/completeness-lambda-calc. Status for the announcement: VERIFIED_SOURCE (precursor, not the published paper; published statements may differ — flag).
- Definitions VERBATIM: "Let C be any CCC (not necessarily with finite limits). Let I_X be the initial CCC generated by a nonempty set X of objects. We say that beta-eta conversion has a COMPLETE INTERPRETATION IN C if there is a faithful CC-functor from I_X to C. We say that beta-eta conversion is COMPLETE FOR C if the class of all CC-functors from I_X to C is collectively faithful."
- VERBATIM: "THEOREM 1. Beta-eta conversion has a complete interpretation in C iff C has an endomorphism all of whose iterates are distinct." / "THEOREM 2. Beta-eta conversion is complete for C iff C is not a preorder."
- Examples VERBATIM: "One category in which beta-eta conversion has a complete interpretation is the category of sets. (This essentially follows from Friedman's completeness theorem. The inclusion of product types makes no significant difference...)" ; "the category of finite sets is complete but has no complete interpretation. (That it has no complete interpretation was observed by Friedman. That it is complete essentially follows from the Plotkin/Statman finite model theorem.)"
- Statman lemma quoted VERBATIM: "THEOREM (Statman [1]). For any sigma there is an L of type sigma -> T such that, for all M, N of type sigma, M =be N iff L(M) =be L(N)." with T = (0→0→0)→(0→0); [1] = Statman, "On the existence of closed terms in the typed lambda-calculus I", To H.B. Curry, Academic Press 1980.
- Follow-up post by Djordje Cubric (6 Jul 1994, same thread) PARAPHRASE: Friedman's theorem implies completeness for free CCCs provided one shows all objects of the free CCC have global support; completeness "holds even when the free ccc has also free arrows among freely generated types."
- Classification: structure = CCC (λ→× ); identity = βη; Theorem 1 gives a SINGLE FAITHFUL CC-functor from the free CCC into ONE fixed concrete category (e.g. Set) — this is the genuine "single fixed model, faithful, structure-preserving" analogue of Kripke completeness, and it holds for ANY CCC with a non-repeating endomorphism. Caveat: free CCC on objects only (no free arrows) in Thm statement; Cubric remark covers free arrows (UNVERIFIED in detail).
- Relevance (INFERENCE): strongest positive datum for the "single fixed non-syntactic model" question, but only for the intuitionistic implicational/conjunctive discipline (cartesian: weakening+contraction). Faithfulness is cheap here (any non-preorder works collectively) — shows "faithful semantic embedding" is a weak constraint, not a discriminating criterion (F02/F08 analogue at proof level).

## S4. Friedman 1975 / Statman 1982 (via Statman & Dowek note)
- Friedman, "Equality between functionals", Logic Colloquium '72–73, LNM 453 (R. Parikh ed.), Springer 1975, pp. 22–37 [biblio as printed in Statman–Dowek ref [1]]: NOT_ACCESSED (original). Statman, "Completeness, invariance and λ-definability", JSL 47(1) 1982, 17–26: NOT_ACCESSED (original).
- Retrieved instead: R. Statman & G. Dowek, "On Statman's Finite Completeness Theorem", arXiv:2309.03602v1 (note dated June 1, 1992; posted 7 Sep 2023). URL https://arxiv.org/pdf/2309.03602. Status: VERIFIED_SOURCE for this note (co-authored by Statman; it reconstructs Friedman/Plotkin and Statman results; §3 "based on Friedman [1], Plotkin [5][6] and Statman [9]").
- Setting: pure simply typed λ→ with one base type ι; "standard model" = full set-theoretic type hierarchy M_{α→β} = M_β^{M_α}; M |= t = u iff equal under every assignment of free variables.
- VERBATIM (§3): "Theorem: (Friedman-Plotkin) Completeness Theorem If t and u are terms of type α then (Mα)α |= t = u if and only if t =βη u." [here M built on M_ι = Λ_ι/=βη with infinitely many variables per type]
- VERBATIM: "Corollary: If ξ is an infinite cardinal and t and u are terms of of type α then Mξ |= t = u if and only if t =βη u." and "So Mξ verifies the completeness theorem if and only if ξ is infinite." (finite-base failure: Church numerals λx:ι.λf:ι→ι.f^n x would need infinitely many distinct values).
- VERBATIM (§5): "Theorem: (Statman) Finite Completeness Theorem Let t be a closed term of type α. There exists a finite model Mt such that Mt |= t = u if and only if t =βη u, and the number of elements of Mι is computable in function of t." Corollary: "t =βη u if and only if for all the finite standard models M, M |= t = u."
- Classification: identity = βη on λ→ (proofs of implicational intuitionistic logic, normal-form identity); Friedman: SINGLE FIXED non-syntactic model (full type hierarchy over any infinite set), complete for all equations (all assignments); Statman finite: per-term finite model (relative completeness), collective over finite models.
- The Friedman-Plotkin proof as presented builds M_ι from SYNTAX (terms mod βη) then transfers to any M_ξ by cardinality (bijection argument) — INFERENCE: the final model is non-syntactic only because the standard hierarchy depends on the base set up to cardinality.

## S8. Soloviev, "Proof of a conjecture of S. Mac Lane"
- BRICS Report RS-96-61, Dec 1996; journal APAL 90 (1997) 101–162 (journal biblio from HHP ref [12], VERIFIED there). URL retrieved: https://www.brics.dk/RS/96/61/BRICS-RS-96-61.pdf (BRICS tech report version). Status: VERIFIED_SOURCE.
- Free SMCC F(A) over a SET OF ATOMS A (objects only; formulas from atoms, I, ⊗, −◦); morphisms = derivations of IMLL (with unit) modulo SMC equality ≡ ("canonical maps").
- Def 2.4 VERBATIM: "Let K be an SMC category. We shall say that K has test-property if for all canonical maps ψ, ϕ : A → B with ψ non-equivalent to ϕ, there exists an assignment J in K such that |ψ|_J ≠ |ϕ|_J." "Thus, K is a complete model with respect to equality in SMC categories."
- Theorem 1 VERBATIM (p.8): "If an SMC category K contains an SMC subcategory V, which satisfies the axioms of test-category in section 10, then K has balanced test-property. If K has biproducts, then it has also full test-property. In particular, the category of vector spaces over a field I has the test-property."
- Theorem 4 VERBATIM: "The SMC subcategory of the category of vector spaces over arbitrary field I, generated by the vector space V = ⊕_{n∈N} I, and by the space 0 satisfies the axioms of test-category." (Uses COUNTABLY-INFINITE-dimensional V; "the principal 'test-arrow' distinguishes between the infinite diagonal matrix id_V ... and the matrixes with a finite number of non-zero rows.")
- Hypotheses/limits: collective over assignments of atoms (objects); free over atoms only (no free arrows/proper axioms); "balanced" sequents (each atom twice, opposite variance) for the general K; history: Szabo's earlier claimed proof flawed; Voreadou's "abstract coherence theorem" had a mistake (counterexample found by Soloviev) and needed a modified description.
- Classification: SMCC / IMLL with unit; identity = SMC (Kelly–Mac Lane) equality; collective completeness in ONE fixed category Vect_k (any field, infinite-dim objects needed in the proof); faithful-family, not one faithful functor.
- Relevance (INFERENCE): concrete-model proof-identity completeness exists for the MILL discipline, but the proof is an intricate, discipline-specific coherence analysis (and a previously published proof was wrong) — evidence against expecting a cheap UNIFORM theorem across structural disciplines (F04).
