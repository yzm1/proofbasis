# Stage 0.6 — Negative obstruction

## 1. Executive verdict

**The frozen claim U is false, conditional on its explicit finite-atomic-interface and fullness requirements.** The obstruction applies to every target satisfying those requirements, not just LF or a particular encoding. A family of finitely presented unary proof calculi has respectively 2, 3, 4, … inequivalent atomic endomorphisms. Full faithful translation must reproduce each of these cardinalities at a native atomic interface. A target with finitely many such interfaces cannot do so.

**Status: INFERENCE — a complete informal argument, not a machine-checked result.** The argument and its potential failure points appear in §3. There is no claim of novelty. Neither finite generation alone nor the broader, parameterized ProofBasis ambition is refuted. The finite-interface condition is an additional hypothesis; a finite operation signature does not imply it. Fullness is stronger than preservation/reflection of equality. Removing either hypothesis materially changes the question.

The strongest countercheck is positive: Thompson's group V has a finite presentation and contains every finite cyclic group. It provides effective compositional, equality-preserving and equality-reflecting embeddings of the entire counterexample family using fixed machinery and no source-specific axioms. These embeddings fail fullness. Thus “incompatible proof identities imply non-finite generation” would be an incorrect conclusion.

**Decision: NARROW.** Stop pursuing U as stated. Keep the more permissive existence question explicitly **UNRESOLVED**, subject to an exact type/interface and trust-boundary decision. The single next mathematical test is the three-element endomorphism problem in §9; it tests a necessary condition for one serious proposed escape rather than another broad benchmark survey.

This investigation reads the integrated Stage-0/0.5 research as evidence, including PR #2's corrections and Report 002's withdrawn claims. It does not modify that research. Source versions and decisive passages are recorded in [005 source notes](005-source-notes.md). **VERIFIED_SOURCE** marks checked primary passages; **INFERENCE** marks the arguments here; **CONJECTURE/UNKNOWN** marks remaining claims. Nothing here has status **FORMALIZED**.

## 2. Exact conjecture examined

### Conjecture freeze (recorded before the proof attempt)

Date: 2026-10-08. This section fixes the provisional interpretation used in this investigation. It is not an amendment to the charter. A user clarification may determine which interpretation is the common target; alternative interpretations will be assessed separately, not substituted into a claimed theorem.

**Source class C.** All finitely schematic calculi as defined in Report 002 §2.1, equipped with independently specified finite schematic presentations of their typed proof congruences. Syntax and rule signatures are finite, sorted, and binding-aware; finite derivations have effectively checkable local steps. Equality is the least congruence generated by the stated equations, not equality of conclusions. Systems need not have decidable proof equality. This is a presentation-theoretic class, chosen independently of any target: it includes the familiar intuitionistic, classical, linear, and modal examples, and simple categorical calculi with proof symmetries. Conclusions must not all be provable in the empty environment. Infinitary proofs and bare cyclic graphs lacking validity witnesses are outside this provisional class.

**Target.** Existentially quantify over a fixed finite sorted typed signature of proof operations, a fixed finite schematic typing/equality presentation, and binding/substitution/composition operations. There are infinitely many generated types and proofs. Checking a finite typing/equality derivation must be effective; deciding equality, proof search, and efficient normalization are not required.

**D2 interface.** Use Report 002's componentwise formula translation, source-independent judgment wrapper, core derivations for rule templates, and no source-specific logical assumptions. Fresh source atoms map to fresh target atoms of a chosen matching sort. For the literal atom-to-atom reading, fix finitely many native atomic judgment interfaces; renaming fresh atoms preserves each interface's typed proof-class structure. An atom cannot secretly become a compound type or acquire a source code, group presentation, universe index, or mode theory. This **atomic-interface condition is an explicit refinement**, not a consequence of a finite operation signature alone.

**Environment.** Translation may rename syntax generators and carry actual source mathematical assumptions/definitions. It may not add proof generators, equations, checker-acceptance assumptions, or logical axioms absent from the source environment. In particular an empty source environment translates to syntax declarations and conservative abbreviations only. Neither an external certificate nor a definition of source derivability authorizes a new proof constructor. Target equality remains fixed.

**Fidelity.** For each S in C, require an effective formula/derivation translation F and fixed core rule templates, natural under both sorted syntax substitution and grafting in proof holes. Require derivability preservation/reflection, composition and binding/substitution compatibility, and

```text
d ~S e  iff  F(d) ≡A F(e).
```

Also require **fullness**: every target inhabitant of a translated open judgment, under exactly the translated environment and boundary hypotheses, is equal to F(d) for some source d. Thus the map on proof classes is a bijection. This strengthening follows Report 002's proposed proof-level gate; embedding without fullness is a separate weaker question.

Resources must be transported according to the source's declared structural discipline; an atomic one-input/one-output linear boundary is a native open interface, not a closed encoded theorem. Pure permutations must preserve the declared independence relation modulo their chosen congruence. Arbitrary β-reduction need not preserve a bijection of raw events: erasure/copying is tracked by residuals where claimed. No one global event order is required across all foundations.

**Frozen claim U.** There exists a target A with these properties such that every (S,~S) in C has such a translation. Quantification is over source presentations fixed independently of A. Uniform algorithmic synthesis of F from S is not required; each F must be effective. The report will distinguish failure of U from failure of a selected encoding and from failure of weaker, parameterized alternatives.

### Why these choices, and which are additional

The source class is presentation-theoretic, not “whatever a chosen algebra encodes.” It uses Report 002 §2.1's finitely schematic systems, with the stated nontriviality restriction and finite schematic proof equations added as explicit data. The examples below are ordinary presentations of categories of proof symmetries. They are small members of a substantial class, not a replacement class. If “foundation” is instead restricted to a named list that excludes them, the argument does not establish impossibility for that list. That restriction must be stated independently.

D1 permits source-rule declarations with proved adequacy; D2 instead requires core-derived rule templates. We examine D2. Report 002's proposed C3 gate and LSR's equational-adequacy template motivate selecting fullness in addition to faithfulness. Fullness says every target proof at a translated boundary comes from the source; faithfulness alone says the translated source proofs remain distinguishable. The original ambition does not automatically mandate fullness. Likewise, native atom-to-atom translation motivates the finite-interface refinement, but neither “typed” nor “finite” alone entails that refinement. These two choices are the smallest additional decisions on which the negative result depends. They were recorded before the argument, not introduced as a repair midway through it.

The formula below isolates the decisive obligations. Write P_S(E;J) for source derivations of the open judgment J modulo the chosen congruence, and P_A(FE;FJ) for target inhabitants modulo fixed target equality:

```text
∃ A [finite fixed signature, typing/equality presentation, and native atomic profiles]
  ∀ S ∈ C  ∃ effective compositional F_S
    ∀ E,J:  F_S : P_S(E;J) ≅ P_A(F_SE;F_SJ),
```

where the bijections preserve identities, composition, the specified substitutions/binders, resources, and the selected independence structure. The symbol ≅ here is a bijection compatible with those operations, not merely equivalence of conclusions. Derivability reflection follows from fullness and preservation at each boundary. The translation may use arbitrarily large finite derived templates: existence is not an efficiency claim. “Finite” counts operation schemas, not proofs, types, or source systems.

## 3. Strongest negative argument: the atomic profile obstruction

### 3.1 Independently presented sources

For each integer n ≥ 2 define S_n. Its formula grammar consists of atoms P, with one atom sort. Context syntax permits an empty boundary or one atomic hypothesis, and judgments have one atomic output. The only inhabited judgments will be linear, unary open judgments `x:P ⊢ d:P`. There is an identity proof `x` and one rule schema, displayed at such a boundary:

```text
x:P ⊢ d:P
------------- rot
x:P ⊢ r(d):P
```

The same rule is schematic at any declared boundary and output; it cannot produce a proof without a premise. There are therefore no closed proofs, no arrows between distinct atoms, and no weakening or contraction. The single proof equation is

```text
rⁿ(ξ) ~ ξ
```

for a typed proof hole ξ. Here rⁿ is literally n occurrences of r. There is no arithmetic checker side condition. Proofs compose by grafting: `d[e/x]`. This is a finite, sorted schematic presentation for each n, with effectively checkable finite derivations. Substitution of atoms by atoms is its sorted formula substitution. If a calculus requires explicit cut syntax, add cut with unit/associativity and its grafting equations; eliminating that notation gives exactly the same presentation.

These are the one-object cyclic-group categories, replicated at fresh atoms. The rule denotes a unary proof symmetry, not a new conclusion or an environmental axiom. The empty mathematical environment remains empty; r and its equation belong to the source's inference/equality machinery. Reclassifying them as target environmental declarations would change the trust ledger. Opaque atomic syntax is permitted by C; the nontriviality condition holds because no atom is provable from an empty boundary.

**Lemma (INFERENCE).** At each atomic boundary, proof classes and composition in S_n form the cyclic group C_n.

*Informal argument.* Induction on derivations gives the representative rᵏ(x) for a unique raw exponent k ≥ 0. Grafting adds exponents. The defining equation reduces an exponent by n whenever possible, so every class has a representative with 0 ≤ k < n. Conversely, interpreting a proof by k modulo n in the additive group Z/n respects identity, grafting, the defining equation, and all congruence rules. Two different residues therefore cannot be equated. There are exactly n classes, with identity residue 0 and inverses represented by residues n−k. This establishes both the count and the composition law without invoking a normalization theorem for a general framework.

### 3.2 Target profiles and the theorem

A *native atomic profile* H_i(A) is the quotient of all target proofs at a one-input/one-output atomic boundary of interface i, in the empty mathematical environment. Fresh atom renaming identifies the profiles within an interface. The profile includes any core constants or derivable operations available at that boundary. It does not restrict target inhabitants to translated proofs. Conservative definitions do not change it. There are no extra source-dependent indices, logical assumptions, or private structural parameters at this boundary. These clauses spell out the frozen interface/environment assumptions; they are not consequences of finite presentation.

**Atomic profile obstruction (INFERENCE).** Let A have k finite native atomic interface choices, with fixed equality and the stated renaming invariance. There cannot be full equality-preserving and equality-reflecting translations of all S_n into those interfaces under empty mathematical environments.

*Informal argument.* For each n, let i_n be the selected interface for a fresh atom. Equality preservation makes the map on proof classes well-defined. Reflection makes it injective, and fullness makes it surjective. Thus

```text
C_n ≅ H_{i_n}(A),     hence |H_{i_n}(A)| = n.
```

Take the k+1 sources S_2 through S_{k+2}. Two choose the same interface, by the pigeonhole principle. Its proof-class set would then have two different finite cardinalities, a contradiction. If k = 0, even S_2 lacks a permitted image. Composition compatibility would also give monoid isomorphisms, but cardinality already suffices. Therefore no existentially chosen A satisfying the frozen hypotheses can establish U. □

This is a class-wide conditional obstruction: the family is fixed before selecting A, and defeats every finite k. It is stronger than observing that a particular LF signature fails. It is **not** an impossibility theorem about every algebra with a finite operation signature. Indeed, the argument would also defeat an infinitely generated target with finitely many atomic profiles and fullness; finite generation itself is not the obstructing invariant.

### 3.3 Attempts to falsify the argument

- **Can translations choose different fresh atom names?** Yes; renaming invariance identifies their profiles. Names cannot alter cardinalities.
- **Can they choose different sorts or resource modes?** Yes, among the k native profiles. Infinitely many indexed modes invalidate the finite-profile hypothesis; they are a genuine alternative, not covered by the theorem.
- **Can a long derived rotation template evade the argument?** No. Its length is unrestricted. It can yield a subgroup with n elements, but fullness concerns all proofs at the boundary, not that subgroup.
- **Can source-specific equations be installed in the target?** Not under fixed equality and the frozen empty-environment policy. Doing so changes the conjecture to an extensible framework with separately supplied proof identities.
- **Is r logically redundant because it has the same premise and conclusion?** At the consequence-relation level, yes. At the selected proof-identity level, no: its n distinct compositions are exactly the data fidelity requires. Rejecting such identities changes the independently declared source class or its equality.
- **Do group inverses imply invalid circular proofs?** No. Each proof is a finite unary derivation. Equality of a finite n-step derivation with identity is not an uncertified cyclic proof graph.

The strongest objection is that fullness plus literal atomic interfaces may overstate the original ambition. That objection limits the theorem's applicability; it does not invalidate its argument. An independent mathematical review is still needed to endorse the informal proof and the modeling of C. No agent agreement is a proof certificate.

## 4. Strongest established relevant theorems and positive argument

### 4.1 Finite presentation does not prohibit all these proof identities

**VERIFIED_SOURCE — Cannon, Floyd, Parry (1996), §6.** V is the group of right-continuous dyadic piecewise-linear bijections of the circle, allowing discontinuities, with slopes powers of 2. The authors describe subgroups isomorphic to finite symmetric groups on dyadic interval partitions (p. 241). Lemma 6.1 gives four generators A, B, C, π₀ and fourteen relations (pp. 241–242). They then define the abstract group V₁ by this presentation. Theorem 6.9 says “V₁ is simple” (p. 248); with the preceding nontrivial surjection V₁ → V, this establishes that the presentation is complete. Lemma 6.1 alone would not suffice to establish a presentation.

A cycle permuting n leaves of a finite binary-tree partition has exact order n. Hence C_n embeds in V for every n ≥ 2. This subgroup statement is also directly reproducible: the n-th power fixes all intervals, while every smaller positive power moves an interval. The finite-generator theorem expresses that cycle by a finite word w_n. For each fixed n, hardcoding such a word gives an effective translation; the frozen conjecture did not require a uniform synthesis algorithm. Equality here is group equality in the finite presentation, not syntactic word equality.

**Positive construction for the obstruction family (INFERENCE).** Let A_V have, at each fresh atom P, the group-word proof operations of V, with composition, identity, inverse, its four generators, and the fixed finite relations. There are no arrows between distinct atoms. This uses an established finite categorical presentation, not a checker operation or source rule declaration. Define

```text
F_n(x) = id_P;     F_n(r(d)) = w_n ∘ F_n(d).
```

The source rule is derived by a fixed word of core operations; w_nⁿ = id is derivable in the core presentation, rather than installed as a new equation. Exact order n establishes preservation and reflection of equality. Grafting becomes composition. Each proof has one input and one output, with no copying or erasure. The source mathematical environment remains empty. Thus one finite presentation handles the **whole S_n family** as faithful compositional embeddings.

The exact failing clause of U is fullness: End_A_V(P) is infinite V, whereas the image at that boundary is the finite subgroup generated by w_n. This construction settles the family under the weaker requirements; it does not establish universality for C. Its operations are group-theoretic proof symmetries, not an asserted complete algebra for mathematical inference.

### 4.2 A verified logical result genuinely includes reflection and fullness

**VERIFIED_SOURCE — Hasegawa (2002), Proposition 5 and Theorem 1, pp. 176–177.** For the specified computational lambda calculus and its linear CPS translation, Proposition 5 states

```text
Γ ⊢ M = N : σ  iff
Γ° ; ∅ ⊢ M° = N° : (σ° → o) ⊸ o.
```

Theorem 1 states that every linear-lambda inhabitant N at such a translated context/type is equal to M° for some source M. The answer base type o must not be a source base type. Proposition 5 credits Sabry–Felleisen; the primary passage checked here is Hasegawa's restatement and his fullness theorem. This is real equality preservation/reflection and fullness across a change of logical discipline, not merely preservation of provability.

It is not a result for arbitrary calculi, classical logic as a whole, or the whole ILL fragment with arbitrary constants. In particular the proof boundary is a compound CPS type, so the theorem supplies evidence for relaxing the literal atomic-interface condition. Its §6 recursion extension states soundness but leaves fullness uncertain in that paper. No inference from one successful translation to unrestricted universality is licensed.

### 4.3 Closest generic construction and its exact gaps

**VERIFIED_SOURCE — Clarke, Scherer, Zeilberger, arXiv:2511.07314v3 (2026), Theorem 1.17.** For an arbitrary functor p:D→C, Λ_p:Bif(p)→C is the free bifibration on p. Its arrows are derivations of their unary sequent calculus modulo the four generating permutation equations of Definition 1.4; cut is admissible and defines composition. The universal property is: for every bifibration q:E→C and functor θ:D→E over C, there is a unique morphism of bifibrations Bif(p)→E extending θ, preserving the chosen cartesian and opcartesian liftings (Lemma 1.16 and the definition on pp. 3–4).

This establishes a mathematically meaningful generative structure, proof composition, and categorical proof identity for that unary bifibrational construction. It does **not** state that every finitely schematic calculus has a full faithful encoding, that the generator functor η_p is full faithful for arbitrary p, or that arbitrary base arrows/equalities can be supplied without source-specific trust. Objects and arrows of C, and generators of D, are parameters: they need not give finitely many native profiles. Therefore this theorem neither proves U nor contradicts §3.

**VERIFIED_SOURCE — Licata, Shulman, Riley (2017), extended version §9.1.** Their positive equational-adequacy template requires derived source rule templates, preserved source equations, a normal-form back-translation with inverse laws, and back-translation of normal-form permutations. But completeness of permutative equality is labeled **Conjecture 8.5** in that version. Remark 9.2 later calls it “Theorem 8.5”; the preceding statement remains a conjecture. The authors explicitly decline to state the template as a lemma because the input class of native sequent calculi is not precisely defined. Its present-day general resolution was not established in this investigation. The 2026 unary theorem above does not discharge all those multiary obligations.

For the **same frozen U**, a generic positive construction would have to derive r_n at a permitted atomic interface, prove exact order n, and prove there are no other endomorphisms for every n. Section 3 shows these obligations cannot all hold. For a parameterized alternative, the exact further obligations are instead: independent specification of admissible parameters; a fixed finite core deriving, rather than importing, source rules and their equations; fully faithful maps of the specified open proof structures; and a uniform account of the selected resource/binding/independence structure. Neither initiality alone nor a finite list of generic rule schemas establishes that package.

## 5. Nontriviality: attacks on the machinery/environment boundary

All attacks below are **INFERENCE**, with their limitations disclosed.

| Attempt | What it buys | Why it fails U; what remains unsettled |
|---|---|---|
| Fixed universal checker with judgment `Accept(code(S),code(J),certificate)` | Effective verification and preservation/reflection of existence of finite source certificates | The translated conclusion is a data-indexed acceptance proposition, not the componentwise core judgment at a native atom. Inference steps execute the encoded rule table. Composition of data constructors does not by itself establish preservation/reflection of selected mathematical proof equality or resource independence. |
| LF-style declaration of one target proof constant per source rule | Often strong adequacy for encoded derivation syntax; HOAS can handle binding | Rule operations are imported as primitives. Adequacy of their representation does not make them derived within the fixed core. This is D1, not the frozen D2 condition. |
| Add `g_P:P→P` and `g_Pⁿ=id` to the environment, calling them facts | Installs exactly the wanted cyclic action | These are absent from the empty source mathematical environment; the source's primitive rule/equality machinery has merely changed address. Classifying trust by whether a declaration quantifies over formulas would miss this specialized instance. |
| Give every source an indexed atom `P[S]`, private mode, or source-dependent wrapper | Arbitrarily many endomorphism profiles | Violates the finite-interface/fixed-wrapper choices. If permitted, it is a genuine alternative: proof is needed that its indices specify mathematical structure rather than a rule/checker table. A finite signature alone cannot make that distinction. |

There is a positive, **non-interpreter** way around finite profiles. In the category of Z-sets, the object Z/n with its regular translation action has exactly n equivariant endomorphisms: a map is determined by f(0)=a, and f(k)=k+a. Composition adds a modulo n, so End(Z/n) ≅ C_n. Distinct object structures supply the different profiles. This elementary argument shows why banning *every* source-dependent mathematical parameter would exclude legitimate mathematics. It does not provide a finite proof calculus deriving arbitrary source rules or prove the whole conjecture.

No tested checker attack satisfies all clauses of U. That is not a theorem characterizing every disguised interpreter. The fixed, native interfaces give a precise invariant excluding the attacks above, but also cause the obstruction. If the project relaxes that invariant, D2's phrases “componentwise” and “mathematically meaningful” are not by themselves a proved general non-interpreter criterion. Admissible structural parameters must be specified positively; source-rule tables cannot simply be renamed “definitions.”

## 6. Structural analysis demanded by the frozen claim

**Composition and substitution.** The obstruction already occurs in the simplest substitution/grafting category, where composition adds exponents. It therefore survives adding further target obligations about binders or richer substitution. Source atoms have their own sort; substituting a compound formula for P is not an operation of S_n. Claiming that they must permit such substitution would change C. Binding-aware calculi elsewhere in C still require their selected capture-avoiding/open-context substitutions; no universal argument for those is supplied by the cyclic example.

**Proof identity and resources.** The sources agree on which atomic unary judgments are derivable; they differ on proof classes. Erasing r as a logically redundant step preserves conclusions and destroys reflection. All proof constructors consume the single boundary input once and produce one output. There is no appeal to contraction/weakening incompatibility, and no conflation of rule-step count with resource usage. Extra target proofs, even if they also use exactly one input, defeat fullness. A single common Cartesian interpretation might collapse distinct linear proofs, but that would only exclude that interpretation, not every finite core with multiple disciplines.

**Causality, independence, and generation.** A raw rᵏ proof has a serial chain of k applications, with no independent pair to permute and no competing redex choices in its construction. The selected equation identifies an n-step chain with identity. Consequently its raw event count and raw chain order are not invariants of its chosen proof identity. U asks for declared independence under pure permutations, not a simultaneous bijection of events under every equation. It imposes no impossible event correspondence on this family. Demanding preservation of raw order under rⁿ=id would make the target specification internally inconsistent for this source, before finite generation was considered.

For actual duplication and erasure, residuals of a proof occurrence may be several or none. Generation and logical dependency must be stated at the relevant derivation/residual level; they cannot generally be recovered as one invariant poset attached to every βη class. Report 003's distinction between CLF traces and stronger proof identity remains essential. Token-level Petri-net independence, concurrency quotients, or resource multiplicities cannot automatically establish the stronger requirement for arbitrary foundations.

**Conflict, nonconfluence, and global validity.** Finitary derivations in C have checked local rule instances. Equality need not be a confluent rewrite relation, and proof search may involve conflict. U does not equate successful proof construction with one confluent execution schedule. Infinitary derivations and uncertified cyclic proof graphs were excluded before the argument; adding finite global-validity certificates requires a fresh explicit presentation and trust audit. Neither their difficulty nor proof-net correctness algorithms imply impossibility of finite generation. The cyclic endomorphism obstruction is independent of those issues.

## 7. Open obligations and failed negative approaches

1. **Finite signature ⇒ finite profiles:** false without a restriction on type/interface formation. Generated compound types and mode indices can yield infinitely many hom-sets. This attempted stronger conclusion was rejected, not smuggled into the theorem.
2. **Infinitely many finite proof identities ⇒ infinitely many primitive operations:** false; §4.1 provides a single finite presentation for faithful embeddings of all the cyclic sources.
3. **Undecidable equality ⇒ impossible effective translation:** invalid. The frozen target need only check finite equality derivations, not decide equality. Similarly, PSPACE-hard proof equivalence, large translation size, or nontermination is not mathematical nonexistence.
4. **Binding cannot be finitely generated:** not established. Existing HOAS/substitution presentations are positive controls; the important gap is whether their imported rule signatures satisfy D2, not whether binding can be represented at all.
5. **Gödel/incompleteness rules out every target:** not established under the permitted mathematical environments. Target theory extensions can carry genuine source assumptions; a fixed empty theory is not the whole conjecture.
6. **Fullness follows from derivability reflection:** false. A_V reflects existence and equality on the cyclic family, while having many extra proofs at each translated boundary.
7. **Generic-framework initiality supplies all required embeddings:** not established. Initiality has a specific quantified model class and generators; arbitrary supplied base arrows or equations may carry precisely the source machinery under investigation.

The unresolved broader problem is not a missing normalization optimization. It is whether an independently admissible class of generated, mathematically structured type interfaces can host the proof categories/multicategories of an independently fixed source class by core-derived operations, with the chosen full or merely faithful maps. There is no general impossibility argument here once both unbounded profiles and faithful embeddings are permitted; nor is there an existence theorem discharging that package.

## 8. What this establishes about ProofBasis

The informal theorem resolves an exact provisional reading of the Stage-0.6 conjecture: **one fixed core cannot provide full proof identities for every finitely schematic source while giving fresh opaque atoms only finitely many native interface profiles**. This conclusion quantifies over all such targets. No selection of additional finite generators, new LF encoding, or faster proof search fixes it.

It does not settle the full ambition in the charter. A construction with richer type translations may remain viable; a faithful embedding can intentionally leave extra target proofs. Both are substantive mathematical changes, not computational efficiency concessions. The positive V result prevents using this report as evidence that all diverse proof identities demand infinitely many primitive operations. The CPS and bifibration results show that strong structural correspondences already exist within precise scopes; they do not establish a universal finite algebra for the broad class.

The practical reduction in uncertainty is a forced choice: retain literal atomic interfaces and fullness and abandon the all-finitely-schematic claim, or explicitly relax at least one of those conditions and accept a new mathematical obligation. No claim that a universal algebra exists, is impossible under every interpretation, or would be novel is justified.

## 9. Decision and the single next mathematical test

**NARROW.** Stop U. Do not implement an algebra or prover. Keep the less restrictive existence question unresolved, with the interface and fullness choices visible rather than silently changing them.

**Next test: the three-element endomorphism problem.** In a precisely fixed, standard propositional dual-context intuitionistic linear lambda calculus with tensor, linear implication, and exponential, with its usual βη/commuting proof equality and no extra axioms or proof constants, determine whether there is a finite type T over fresh atoms such that

```text
End(T) = {id, ρ, ρ²},       ρ³ = id,       id ≠ ρ ≠ ρ² ≠ id,
```

where End(T) comprises **all** classes of derivations `x:T ⊢ t:T` in the linear context. Give a core term for ρ and prove the displayed exhaustive classification, or prove no such T exists. The precise calculus and equality must be fixed before attempting the classification; “ILL” alone is not that specification. Status of the test here: **UNKNOWN**, not claimed novel.

This is a necessary condition for using that core to fully faithfully represent S_3 after relaxing atom-to-atom translation. Its success does not establish U or universality; its failure would exclude that core for the broader class even with compound atom images. It directly tests a possible repair to the exact obstruction, instead of collecting translations unrelated to it.

A targeted search found Soloviev's 2019 work on type automorphism groups, including advertised finite-group representations at second-order/dependent types. Its exact theorem hypotheses were not recovered here; see the access limits in the source notes. It is a relevant existing lead, not evidence that the test is novel. Automorphism representation alone also does not classify every endomorphism as required by fullness.

A cheap first subcase is already informative (**INFERENCE**, restricted to the free symmetric monoidal tensor-only proof calculus on one atom). End(P) is trivial; End(P⊗P) is the two-element permutation group; End(P⊗P⊗P) is S_3, which contains a three-cycle but also three transpositions. Generally End(P^⊗m) is S_m, by following labeled inputs through tensor decomposition/reassembly and quotienting associativity/unit/exchange coherence. No factorial equals 3. Thus simply using more tensor copies gives an order-three operation without the required fullness. This is not asserted as a theorem about arbitrary higher-order/exponential types, nor about all inhabitants of an unspecified larger ambient calculus. The expensive part of the next test is exactly their complete endomorphism classification.

**Final recommendation: NARROW.**
