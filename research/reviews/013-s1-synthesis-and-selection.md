# S1 synthesis and S2 selection — 2026-10-13

**Status:** Coordinator synthesis and proposed stage-gate decision, not a new mathematical theorem. Sources: [landscape audit 011](011-landscape-audit.md) from PR #19; [strategy audit 012](012-strategy-options.md) from PR #18; earlier reports, especially 003, 006–010. Primary-source claims in the audits remain subject to independent verification.

## Executive decision

**Accept S1 breadth and strategic alternatives as adequate to select bounded S2 work, but do not claim a complete literature survey or approve a universal finite-algebra conjecture.**

Select a **three-part comparison program**:
1. **CMP-01 shared contract:** define comparable proof/judgment/context/identity/trust interfaces, using existing mathematics and without inventing an anti-interpreter predicate.
2. **Two complementary, bounded probes:** ALG-01 (binding-aware generators, categorical proof structure, presentation equivalence) and INST-01 (generic mode/doctrine frameworks, LSR proof-equivalence conjecture and indexed contexts).
3. **OBS-01 adversarial classification:** compare finite-generation/coherence obstruction results to the same contract, ensuring a proposed universality result does not follow from a coding trick.

Not yet commissioned: proof-theoretic harmony deep dive, HoTT/cubical, multiway causal simulation, graph infrastructure, and new prover implementation. They remain on the landscape with explicit triggers.

## Reconciled findings: where reports 011 and 012 agree

- The original 'all mathematics from a finite rule list' is already tractable at derivability/representation level but insufficiently explanatory.
- A fixed universal algebra is one *hypothesis*; a family of theories, universal-property structures or a classification can be meaningful outcomes.
- Presentation equivalence, proof identity and trusted inference content cannot be reduced to primitive names or proof-checker expressiveness.
- Polygraphs and higher rewriting supply useful language for generators, relation cells and coherence. Finite generators do not imply finite convergent/coherent presentations.
- The LSR/generic mode framework and published Conjecture 8.5 are important uncompleted prior-art checks. Do not assert the conjecture remains open in 2026 without fresh primary-source verification.
- Notions of finite basis/irreducibility and proof-theoretic justification are distinct: clones/Lawvere theory describe generation, while harmony/admissibility address rule legitimacy.
- Higher identity (HoTT/cubical), causal event structures and multiway rewriting are plausible enrichments, not proven universal primitives.

## Shared benchmark obligations

Every selected approach must address, or explain why out of scope: (i) intuitionistic ND βη, (ii) classical proof identity with *declared* choice, (iii) linear/resource rules and MLL net permutation, (iv) dependent binding/substitution, (v) ordinary rewriting/coherence, (vi) cyclic global validity as boundary, (vii) checker/typed-path and group encodings as non-vacuity controls, (viii) partial-order causality *only if the approach advertises it*. Distinguish proven correspondence, counterexample and unsupported feature.

## Reconciliation of divergent priorities

Audit 011 ranks a specific LSR identity conjecture first (DD0). Audit 012 asks for common comparison semantics before selecting a target. **Resolution:** do CMP-01 first, but allow parallel retrieval/verification of LSR status; no proposed theorem can be promoted until its source/target equivalence and trusted parameters match the contract.

Audit 011 highlights Guiraud finite 3-polygraph and higher-dimensional rewriting. Audit 012 ranks generation theories, indexed frameworks and obstruction research. **Resolution:** include Guiraud and polygraphs explicitly in ALG-01 as a comparison/negative control; authorize a standalone higher-rewriting deep dive only if the algebraic comparison exposes an exact gap.

Neither report licenses replacing the cross-foundation ambition with propositional-only fragments. Those are reference cases, not the definition of universality.
