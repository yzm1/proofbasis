## 2. Repaired contract: what an opposing investigator must test

### 2.1 Typed interfaces and composition

Write `Pr_S(E;b)` for **valid derivations modulo the specified source equality** at a well-formed boundary `b`. Raw derivations and validity are supplied separately. An environment has four marked parts: object declarations; transparent definitions with expansion witnesses; assumed mathematical evidence with its full boundary; and additional trusted rule/equality declarations. Empty and hypothetical contexts are different boundaries.

A comparison comprises `(T_E,T_C,T_J,T_H,F)` and specified boundary isomorphisms where strict preservation is inappropriate. `T_C` acts on contexts **and substitutions**; `T_H` acts on scoped proof holes. No map silently forgets the premises of an assumption `u:(Γ ⊢ A)`: it remains a contextual parameter or an appropriately typed abstracted arrow. A target declaration of closed `u:T(A)` would strengthen that environment.

For NJ use a cartesian multicategory: arrows `Γ → A`, simultaneous substitution into hypotheses, and the cartesian action on contexts. Identity, associativity, exchange, weakening and contraction are explicit structural schemas. Abstraction discharges one named hypothesis. Both object/formula substitution and proof-hole substitution must commute with `F`.

For classical MLL use a **symmetric polycategory**, with arrows `Γ → Δ`, represented by nets on `Γ^⊥,Δ`. Cut composes along one matching formula occurrence. Other occurrences remain distinct; there is no diagonal or deletion action. This is not cartesian simultaneous substitution. Multiple grafts require disjoint linear resources and the polycategory interchange laws. The frozen cut-free derivations alone are not closed under cut: composition means insert cut and eliminate it on nets, obtaining a cut-free representative. Independence of that representative modulo the frozen net equality is a required lemma, not a definition of raw cut-free tree grafting.

For all admissible substitutions `σ:Δ→Γ` and all well-typed hole fillings `θ`, require

```text
F(id) = id;
F(q ∘_A p) = F(q) ∘_{T(A)} F(p);
T_C(id) = id;  T_C(σ τ) = T_C(σ) T_C(τ);
F(d[σ;θ]) = F(d)[T_C(σ);F(θ)].
```

These are equations in the *fixed target congruence*, with declared structural isomorphisms inserted if necessary. A decoder used to define composition has to satisfy these equations and typing; successful closed-proof recompilation is insufficient.

### 2.2 Two reflection contracts, and three different equality obligations

Fix the target calculus, target validity, exact translated environment, and boundary translation **before** any reflection claim.

| Obligation | Quantifiers / test |
|---|---|
| P | For every `E,b`, nonempty `Pr_S(E;b)` implies nonempty `Pr_T(T_E E;T b)`; `F` supplies the proof. |
| **R_intr** | For every `E,b`, nonempty **entire** `Pr_T(T_E E;T b)` implies nonempty `Pr_S(E;b)`. Interior target proofs may use every rule of the declared target. |
| **R_rel(I)** | For a separately given equality-stable target subinterface `I(E;b)`, nonempty `I(E;b)` implies nonempty `Pr_S(E;b)`. Its typing, closure under permitted composition/substitution, and acceptance predicate are part of the statement. |
| EP | For all same-boundary raw source proofs `d,e`, `d ≈_S e` implies `F(d) ≈_T F(e)`. This is needed to define `F` on proof classes. |
| ER | For all such `d,e`, `F(d) ≈_T F(e)` implies `d ≈_S e`. This is injectivity on proof classes, not existence reflection. |
| F_intr | For every target class in the entire translated hom-set there exists a source class mapping to it. With EP+ER this gives a hom-set bijection. |
| F_rel | The corresponding surjectivity only onto `I`. |

`R_rel(im F)` is automatic from a total translation and is recorded as **tautological**, with no explanatory weight. A nontrivial relative theorem must specify an independently recognizable target fragment and its decoder/adequacy argument. It is still weaker than intrinsic reflection.

A syntactically translated boundary may carry modes, compound types, or indices. “Intrinsic” means no *further* proof-image restriction after that boundary is frozen; it does **not** mean “cannot encode the source in its type.” Report 009's checker therefore can satisfy R_intr at its coded boundary. This explicitly resolves C1 without pretending to repair non-vacuity by renaming reflection.

Source equality and target equality are judgment-indexed congruences. A target proof of an internal equality proposition is not target conversion. Neither source equality reflection nor source identity preservation follows from truth preservation, freeness, normalization, or raw LF adequacy alone. Fullness implies R_intr when P is given; ER does not. No fullness is assumed outside a claim that names it.

### 2.3 Resource, environment, trust and presentation contracts

**U is a context-action contract.** The mode map specifies permissions for each hypothesis block; structural actions and splitting diagrams commute with `F`. For the direct translations below a linear occurrence remains one linear occurrence; nonlinear slots retain cartesian permissions. Raw term occurrence counts are not an NJ βη invariant: `(λx.y)u = y` erases `u`. No U claim here says those raw histories survive. In MLL the surviving observation is named boundary occurrences and axiom linking, invariant under the chosen permutations.

**TR is relative to a marked base, not an unannotated proof category.** Supply a base doctrine `D`, a map of doctrines where applicable, and a ledger for object and proof declarations. An operation is **derived relative to D** when an open, scoped D-proof template realizes it using exactly its premise holes and recorded dependencies. An admissibility theorem only preserving closed provability is not automatically such a template, and an arbitrary constant of the right type is not a derivation from the empty ledger. A new equality declaration similarly needs a derivation in the old congruence or must be counted as new trusted structure.

A finite rule table is allowed as **declared theory data in DI**; its entries remain trusted generators. It is never counted as derivation of those rules in one unchanged core. Atom declarations do not authorize new inhabitants of those atoms. SC allows no such imported logical rule data.

Allowed presentation changes are effective, sort/mode/ledger-preserving definitional equivalences: add `r = t` with a typed old-language template and its defining equation; add a proved equation; inline either; rename; or give explicitly inverse context/substitution-preserving translations. Adding a derivable rule **without** its defining equation may create new proof classes. Moving an assumed rule to an unmarked assumption type is not ledger-preserving. Morita equivalence of model categories alone is insufficient to transport hom-set identities, marked sorts, or trust. Arbitrary sort additions require an explicit contextual equivalence; no universal dependent Tietze theorem is assumed.

Existence of a finite presentation in this fixed doctrine can survive these changes. The displayed number or names of primitive operations do not. A checker cannot be excluded merely because the first presentation spells an operation `check`.

## 3. The fixed controls, with equations and actual maps

### NJ: implication/product natural deduction

Atoms `p`; formulas `p | A⇒B | A×B`. Terms: hypotheses, `λx.t`, application, pairs and projections; α-renaming is implicit. The least typed congruence has function β, function η with the freshness condition, projection β for both projections, and product η. Contexts allow exchange, weakening and contraction. Empty contexts are retained; a terminal **context object** need not add a unit connective to the source language.

Interpret terms as arrows in the freely generated cartesian closed structure: `λ` is transpose of evaluation, application is evaluation after pairing, and pairs/projections are the product operations. The inverse is the internal term interpretation. The equations in both directions are exactly the βη laws and structural substitution laws. Restrict the free CCC with terminal object to the unit-free source types; conservativity of adding the terminal type at these boundaries is a separate, standard normalization argument. This qualification avoids falsely identifying the unit-free syntax with the entire CCC language.

**Witnesses:** `π₁⟨u,v⟩ = u` and `λx.fx = f` translate to the matching product/exponential equations, not a generic certificate-irrelevance axiom. Raw LF constructors `impI`, `impE` do not inherit object β: `impE(impI(λx.x),u)` can remain a constant-headed LF normal form.

### MLL: cut-free, unit-free, no mix

Formulas are literals `p,p^⊥`, tensor and par, with involutive De Morgan negation. Boundaries have named occurrences; exchange acts on those names. Rules are atomic axiom, tensor with disjoint context split, and par. Compound identity is its η-expanded derivation. Equality is the congruence generated by the tensor/par commutations in the multiplicative restriction of van Glabbeek–Hughes Tables 2–4, with the same boundary tracking. Equivalently, equal translated linkings/nets; no mix is added.

The translation retains the formula forest, creates the axiom link at each axiom, joins tensor premises, and adds a par node. Define valid target nets independently: every leaf belongs to exactly one dual atomic axiom link, and every switching (retain one premise edge at each par) is connected and acyclic. No mix, units, or generalized trusted axiom boxes are present. Composition connects dual distinguished ports by cut and normalizes the net. Resource identity is axiom linking on named leaves, not the chronology of a sequent proof. Validity implies sequentializability by [G], whereas equality alone does not prove that arbitrary graph structures are proofs. In §6's family specialization one may instead use the quotient sequent presentation directly; the graph presentation needs this separate theorem. The multiplicative cut-reduction procedure has a unique cut-free result by [G] Theorems 3–4 restricted to this additive-free fragment, giving well-defined net composition.

**Witness:** take `ax(p)` and `ax(q)`, tensor their positive occurrences to get `p^⊥,q^⊥,p⊗q`, and an `ax(r)` premise. Compare applying par to `p^⊥,q^⊥` before the final tensor of `p⊗q` with `r`, versus after that tensor. Both conclude `p^⊥⅋q^⊥,r^⊥,(p⊗q)⊗r`, and have identical axiom links. A rule-constructor encoding distinguishing these trees fails EP for this frozen equality.

**Resource negative:** `p ⊢ p⊗p`, i.e. `⊢p^⊥,p⊗p`, has no axiom matching of all leaves. The cartesian diagonal proves its naive tensor-to-product translation. This rejects that translation's R_intr/U, not all modal or LNL encodings.

## 5. An obstruction that closes one definition path

**INFERENCE, elementary; already the substance of 009 and 010.** Fix any source proof interface `P`, and consider triples `(P,B,F)` with typed composition/substitution and any retained resource annotations. Let `N` be a predicate invariant under isomorphisms of such triples. For any two realizations `F₁:P→B₁` and `F₂:P→B₂` which are structured isomorphisms, `H=F₂F₁⁻¹` is a structured isomorphism with `HF₁=F₂`. Therefore

```text
N(P,B₁,F₁) = N(P,B₂,F₂).
```

For every finite graph `G`, the checker and path interfaces in §3 meet these hypotheses by flattening. Consequently **no such N can accept the native path realization and reject the checker realization**. This remains true after adding any structure transported along H. It does not quantify over the whole ambient target, a designated base map, or untransported provenance: those are extra data, and their preservation must be checked separately.

Thus R_intr, ER, fullness, reconstruction, representability, and connective universal properties alone are fidelity conditions, not a non-vacuity definition. Adding more interface-invariant adjectives cannot solve this example. Nor is merely excluding a data sort invariant: 010 operational §3 derives a finite multiplication table inside ordinary logical case templates, and §6 adds a harmless defined K combinator that defeats a literal primitive-form restriction.

**Repair with an explicit cost:** SC fixes its ambient doctrine and permits no table parameter; DI marks the variable doctrine and its generators as trusted. These are reproducible hypotheses about the question being asked. They are not a theorem that every other representation is meaningless. No candidate is called non-vacuous because it “is structurally faithful” or “does not look like a checker.”
