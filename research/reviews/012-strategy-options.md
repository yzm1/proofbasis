## 1. Verdict and recommendation

**Do not presently organize the research around proving the existence of one finite universal algebra.** Organize the next comparison around **generating theories and their structure-preserving translations**, with an obstruction/classification track running alongside. Treat the single-algebra claim as one possible later outcome, not the admission criterion for useful mathematics. This is a PROPOSAL, not a charter change.

The strongest current combination is:

1. **Presentation-invariant bases:** typed/binding-aware operation theories, and explicitly chosen equivalences between presentations.
2. **Indexed relations between foundations:** generic modal/resource machinery, contextual or fibrational structure, and separate trust obligations for parameters.
3. **Limits and classification:** identify exactly which translation contracts are possible, which properties are lost, and which extra structure distinguishes interpretations.

Universal-property and higher-dimensional methods support these tracks. Causal models are worth a bounded comparison where events are meaningful. Proof interoperability is a legitimate separately labelled result, but does not establish fundamental proof machinery.

There are substantial established positive results: binding-aware presentation/theory correspondences [F], a fully faithful and full Girard translation on its specified fragment [H], generic identity and cut for mode-indexed calculi [M], free categorical completions recorded in Report 003, and coherent completions for convergent rewriting [P]. They do not compose into the proposed universal theorem. The issue is not simply that “proofs are too different”; their **comparison structures, equalities and trust contracts differ**.

The largest uncertainty is what information a common theory is supposed to explain **beyond representing a selected proof-class interface**. Reports 009 and both 010 reviews make this concrete: some native path and checker interfaces are isomorphic; N_log changes verdict under conservative generator changes; finite menus do not prevent table data in constant images. A universal object could therefore exist and still answer the wrong explanatory question.

**Recommendation:** PROCEED with bounded S1/S2 comparison, keep universal existence attempts suspended, and accept a classification, known-theorem identification, or defensible negative result as success. Do not commission all directions below at once.

## 2. What is being compared

The objective remains understanding the generative machinery of mathematical proving across foundations. The alternatives change the mathematical formulation of that objective; they do not quietly change “across foundations” to one favored fragment.

Keep three layers separate:

* **Generation:** operations with typed inputs, binders, contexts and composition; equations and higher transformations between composites.
* **Justification:** why a rule is licensed—assumption, derived macro, admissibility theorem, universal property, soundness theorem or additional trusted axiom. These are different obligations.
* **Representation:** the particular preservation/reflection theorem for a map between independently specified systems.

Finite generation is an attribute that may be investigated at each layer. It is not a definition of fundamentality. Finitely many operation schemas do not imply finitely many types, finitely many interfaces, decidable equality, a finite homotopy basis, or efficient normalization.

For comparison, use a **versioned contract**, not a new universal conjecture: source judgment/context sorts; source equality; substitution/binding; resource modes; environment premises/provenance; target boundary; translation laws; and separately any event or higher-equivalence obligations. The benchmark list tests contracts and diagnoses missing structure. A finite list cannot establish universality: a disjoint union of its presentations is an obvious nonexplanatory control.

The common controls remain NJ βη; classical reasoning with a specified equality; linear/resource-sensitive reasoning; dependent binding; equational rewriting; cyclic/global validity; proof-net permutations; group presentations; and explicit checkers. Include a positive witness and an adversarial witness for each advertised property. A control may reveal a limitation without disqualifying an entire mathematical tradition.

## 4. Mathematical reasons and failure modes

### 4.1 A single core versus families of bases

**INFERENCE.** A unique list of “fundamental generators” is not required to understand generation. NAND alone and conjunction/negation give different presentations of Boolean operations; the elementary identities are `¬x=NAND(x,x)` and `x∧y=NAND(NAND(x,y),NAND(x,y))`. This example concerns truth functions, not proof objects, but already refutes using a favored primitive list as the invariant.

The proof-relevant k-extension in Report 010 operational review is stronger: adding `k=λx.λy.x` with its defining equation preserves every typed operation class and substitution, while literal N_log changes verdict. Adding the same generator **without** its defining equation adds proof classes. Report 010 algebraic review corrected precisely this distinction. Thus a basis program must record equations and which presentation changes are permitted; “adding derived rules is harmless” is false without that qualification.

Rank, existence of a finite presentation and existence of finite coherent data can remain invariant even when a named primitive basis does not. Neither report proves a general Tietze/Morita theorem covering dependent sorts, resource zones and provenance. That is an exact gap suitable for B, not evidence that all fundamentals vanish under encoding. Ordinary cartesian clones implicitly allow projections/reuse; a linear counterpart must encode context discipline rather than infer it from syntax.

A hierarchy need not claim all foundations have equivalent bases. Its objects can be presentations modulo an explicitly chosen equivalence; its arrows are the selected structured interpretations. “S admits an interpretation in T” gives a comparison preorder only after identities and composition of the contracts are verified. Mutual faithful embeddings alone must not be called equivalence of theories. The resulting incomparabilities would themselves explain limits of common machinery. Selecting this object is a proposal for comparison, not a frozen universal target.

There are conditional obstacles to A, not a general negative theorem. Report 005's finite-atomic-profile/fullness argument fails when either hypothesis is dropped; faithful cyclic embeddings into Thompson's V and compound tensor interfaces are explicit positive counterchecks. Reports 006–007 use an undecidable group word problem to rule out **effective equality-reflecting translation into a decidable target equality**, not finite generation itself. These limits make a classification program more immediately defensible than “one algebra or nothing.”

### 4.2 Universal properties and indexed frameworks

Universal properties explain operations through their behavior under composition, rather than constructor names. But the doctrine, probes, variances and equality being classified must be fixed. A free CCC classifies cartesian proof operations; it does not make a diagonal a valid linear proof. Similarly, [I]'s theory colimit combines specifications, not a derivation of their inference rules from one trusted core.

[M] is the strongest reason to prioritize an indexed approach: fixed generic F/U machinery derives cut and identity over variable mode theories. The extended text also explicitly conjectures completeness of its permutative equality. Structural parameters therefore have mathematical content and exact proof obligations. Calling all per-foundation structural data “imported inference” prematurely discards a serious candidate; calling all such data “harmless environment” prematurely accepts an interpreter. Trace what is assumed, computed, and generically derived.

Plain institutions supply sentence/model functors and satisfaction under signature change, not a chosen set of proof objects. Given any proof-relevant calculus, replacing every nonempty parallel proof set by a singleton can preserve consequence while destroying proof identity. Thus even a perfect institutional satisfaction translation need not be R3-faithful. A proof-relevant fibration/general-logic enrichment must carry the missing data and establish its own translation laws. Fibrations are not automatically neutral about contraction, dependent context extension or computation.

The co-Kleisli/cartesian link also needs hypotheses: Report 010 algebraic source notes recover Benton's products condition. Do not generalize “a comonad explains linear/nonlinear reasoning” to an arbitrary comonad or all closed categories. [V] independently shows that value/computation/stack sorting and adjunctions can unify meaningful constructions without first selecting an undifferentiated universal algebra. Its particular models have countable products/coproducts; this is not a finitary-source theorem.

### 4.3 Higher dimensions, proof identity and causal histories

There are at least three different objects: a raw derivation; a class modulo selected proof equations; and a transformation witnessing an equality or reduction. Quotienting the third into the second loses information. Retaining it in a polygraph may expose which equations/coherences have finite generators. [P] gives an established technique and genuine limitations: finite convergent presentations have finite derivation type; its stated examples have decidable word problem and finite presentation but no finite derivation type or finite convergent presentation. This defeats “decidable equality means finite normalizing/coherent basis,” not the broad finite-core conjecture.

[W]'s identity-type towers carry weak ω-category structure using structural and identity rules. This is important existing common machinery for higher identities. It does **not** assert that cut elimination, nonconfluent rewriting, or proof search forms that same object. Identity paths may be invertible in suitable higher structures; an erasing reduction is not an invertible event map. Strictly identifying all higher transformations, or requiring their unique normal forms, needs a separate theorem.

For causal semantics, `a→b` and `a→c` with distinct normal endpoints are alternatives in conflict, not two independent orders. Also `(λx.a)b→a` removes the b-argument's events; duplicating substitution creates multiple residuals. A fixed event bijection cannot descend through all β-equations. [E]'s prime representation has precise domain/enabling assumptions, and more general stable/enabling structures accommodate distinctions a single poset loses. A cyclic proof's finite back-edge graph is not itself an acyclic causal order; its unfolding and global trace witness are additional objects.

A multiway graph is consequently a useful operational **presentation**, not by itself a proof identity, validity theorem or generative explanation. Wolfram-style multiway/ruliad proposals should be tested against typed rewrite/event semantics with the same controls. No original result uniquely supporting those proposals was verified here; defer claims of added explanatory power until a discriminating theorem is supplied. Generic graph exploration can record every possible rewrite while saying nothing about which paths are justified mathematical proofs.

### 4.4 Other serious alternatives and what they add

**Synthetic rules/focusing (J).** The question “what are the meaningful steps?” may have a normalization answer: phases, not individual constructors. [Q] identifies maximally multi-focused proofs modulo iso-polarity with nets in its unit-free MLL fragment. This is stronger than completeness of focused search, but deliberately scoped. It offers a mature test for whether generators should be macro-operations and whether proof permutations are presentation noise. It cannot be generalized to arbitrary classical, dependent or cyclic equality without new results.

**Full/faithful semantic characterization (K).** [H]'s erasure and logical-predicate argument identifies precisely which linear-target maps come from source proofs. Report 003 records further net/game correspondences. A model separating proofs establishes faithfulness; it need not reflect derivability because it may add morphisms such as the diagonal `p→p⊗p`. Report 004's withdrawn one-point-frame test conflated these roles. Its replacement uniform-family/fullness question may be worth a semantic dive only after checking that its uniformity and finite-frame restrictions are independently justified and not already answered. Do not silently adopt its narrower P₃ as the project target.

**Algebraization (Z).** Abstract algebraic logic is missing as a distinct family in the seed map. Its object is a consequence relation and its algebraic semantics, not an algebra of proof terms. It could classify which foundations admit particular algebraic presentations and distinguish genuine semantic correspondence from mere code interpretation. Blok–Pigozzi's *Algebraizable Logics* is a primary reading lead; its exact characterization theorems were not retrieved/verified in this audit. No result from it is used as a decisive claim. The thin-quotient example above already explains why consequence algebraization cannot automatically preserve proof identities.

**Additional boundary leads, not commissioned dives:** proof-theoretic strength/relative interpretability and reflection hierarchies, and proof-complexity simulations. They address whether an imported mathematical principle changes justified strength and how much structural compilation costs. An ordinary conditional axiom environment is not the same as an unproved inference-rule import. Polynomial simulation is not equality-faithful representation. Exact incompleteness, ordinal-analysis or simulation theorems must be verified before making them a gate; none is asserted here to refute universal finite machinery. Operads/PROPs/polycategories and categorical semantics belong in B/C/E, with multiple input/output and noncartesian structure retained. Homological invariants have a concrete precedent in [P]; general topology or Langlands analogies without specified proof objects remain lower priority.

### Was anti-interpreterness premature?

**As a warning and control: no. As the first universal admission predicate: yes.** Universal computation should never count as success at the north star. But prior work tried to exclude all interpreters before fixing what a successful structural explanation would provide. The required rejection of every nonlogical table then conflicted with accepting legitimate relative representations of groups and theories; Report 010 algebraic review explicitly flags this owner decision. Even parametric natural transformations can encode nonlogical actions, and an interface isomorphism can hide different implementations.

The remedy is not to approve vacuous solutions. Keep the trust boundary and reject a checker-only existence proof as fulfillment of the goal. **Compare** it nonetheless: identify which extra typed operations, universal properties, coherence laws, residual behavior or justification witnesses the explanatory theory supplies. If those additions cannot distinguish any meaningful mathematical behavior or admitted presentation change, report that limitation. Do not protect the intended answer by relabelling failed controls after the fact.

## 6. Largest uncertainties and proposed decisive sequence

These proposals are research tasks, not new frozen statements. Proceed sequentially through gates; retrieval within an accepted task can be parallel.

### Gate 1 — comparison semantics before preferred targets

Prepare one contract sheet for each common control with exact proof equality, contextual substitution, resource operations, hypothetical environment premises, and whether equality is definitional or internal evidence. Select permitted changes of presentation: adding a derived generator with its equation; adding a defined sort; expansion of a connective; change in resource mode. Classical equality and dependent type conversion must not be guessed to match the intuitionistic case.

**Discriminating test:** compare the k-extension, a defined connective and a defined sort against the checker/path isomorphism. Does the chosen extra structure survive the honest extensions and still distinguish the two implementations when that distinction is demanded? Outcome may be “no invariant of this enriched interface can do so.” That is useful information about the objective. **Stop:** if there is no decision about what explanatory data must be retained, do not commission existence proofs.

### Gate 2 — two complementary mathematical dives

**Dive B/D:** compare binding-aware operation theories and a generic mode/context framework on the same intuitionistic/linear fixtures, plus a dependent-family substitution boundary. Recover [M]'s published equality conjecture and current follow-up status. Determine whether a stated correspondence is already known, and exactly which trust/resource structures its morphisms preserve. A restricted witness is a diagnostic, not evidence for unrestricted universality.

**Dive G/E:** independently audit finite generation versus finite relations/coherence using [P] and the repository's conditional group/profile obstruction. Identify which invariant defeats **every** allowed presentation/translation and which only defeats finite normalization, a native interface, or one encoding.

**Expected decision value:** either an established common mathematical object/bridge becomes available, or one exact structural incompatibility makes the intended universal contract untenable. **Stop/re-route:** an existing theorem subsumes the local target; a decisive missing hypothesis invalidates the proposed inference; or the only remaining work is terminological repackaging. Consult current specialists before assuming an old published conjecture is open.

### Gate 3 — targeted mismatch tests, only where material

* **Normative mismatch:** harmony/definability/admissibility with a tonk-like negative control and the roll/unroll definition. Determine which policy is a justified rule discipline and which merely selects primitive syntax.
* **Higher/causal mismatch:** a commuting-rule square, an erasing β-step and a conflict fork. Test an event-to-proof-quotient comparison with explicit residuals; use cyclic/global trace validity as a boundary, not as a complexity-based impossibility shortcut.
* **Semantic mismatch:** a linear diagonal versus an authentic proof, and a chosen classical proof equality. Verify definability/fullness only if needed for the explanatory comparison; do not reintroduce fullness as a universal requirement because one model has extra maps.

**Expected value:** determine whether one description must be replaced by a layered/higher object, or whether causal/normative structure should be a separate indexed component. **Stop:** no new distinction beyond known representability, or the claim depends on an unapproved source-class change.

### Gate 4 — integration and possible S3 return

Integrate exact theorem statements and counterexamples into a preservation/obstruction classification. Require at least one comparison crossing substantive foundations, and an explicit account of the remaining dependent, classical and cyclic boundaries. Do not infer a theorem from passing the finite suite. Only then propose a universal, hierarchy, free-object, or classification conjecture—with competing alternatives and falsifiers. This task does not authorize that freeze.

## 8. Conditions for returning, stopping, or succeeding differently

Return to a finite-universal-algebra conjecture only if all of the following can be proposed concretely for review:

1. An independently specified substantial **parameterized** source class; the benchmark alone is insufficient. Exclusions and foundation boundaries are explained.
2. A mathematically defined core/context/trust interface preserving hypothetical premises and substitution—not only constructor names.
3. A stable notion of basis/translation equivalence that survives the admitted conservative presentation changes.
4. An explanatory property beyond checker representation, with an actual positive witness and adversarial negative witness. Any deliberate acceptance of nonlogical table interpretations is recorded, not hidden.
5. Evidence that sharing generic operations explains more than a disjoint union of the benchmark systems; no assumption that finite signatures imply finite interfaces or decidable equality.
6. A theorem-shaped target not already subsumed by existing mathematics, or an explicit decision to treat recovery of that known theorem as success. Current literature status must be checked.
