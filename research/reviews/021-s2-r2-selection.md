## Frozen proposition and distinction

**SC** is the proposition in report 017 §6/Card SC, as critically restated in report 020 §8, with **fixed D***, full Shulman §8 target congruence, disjoint nonlinear/linear atom sets X,Y and *canonical* source maps. It requires at every pure source boundary a bijection between NJ beta-eta or unit-free no-MIX cut-free MLL net proof classes and **all** target classes at the translated pure boundary, naturally in typed substitution/composition. This is intrinsic fullness plus equality preservation/reflection, not merely a modal-free-image representation.

**D_MLL** is the more restricted bridge N→T_0: it may be a pure-case lemma/prior-art identification, but is not by itself SC, because SC also tests the map T_0→A* into the larger mixed grammar. Document target objects, equality, units, modes and connective maps for each arrow. Do not silently replace one with the other.

## Mathematically decisive failure tests

1. A well-typed proof in the full mixed target at a translated pure sequent that has no source proof refutes intrinsic reflection.
2. A well-typed mixed target proof class at a source-provable pure boundary not equal to any image refutes intrinsic fullness.
3. Two distinct source MLL axiom matchings identified in mixed target congruence refute equality reflection.
4. A source beta-eta or MLL rule commutation not respected by the map refutes equality preservation.
5. A modal F/U or unit detour must be shown typable and distinct under a separating model or converted using the **complete** published congruence; merely displaying a detour is not a counterexample.

All negative results would target this specific D* and its canonical maps—not every possible finite algebra. Do not reclassify them as universal impossibility.

## Prior art requiring precise matching

- Heijltjes–Straßburger, *Proof nets and free semi-star-autonomous categories*, freeness of unit-free nets with virtual-unit structure: verify source quotient, units and comparison object.
- Shulman, *Star-Autonomous Envelopes and Conservativity* (2021), including Theorem 10.1 and any hypotheses about existing products/limits; this does not automatically prove conservativity when F/U and nonlinear Cartesian operations are simultaneously added.
- Shulman, *LNL polycategories and doctrines of linear logic* (2023), Theorem 7.4, §8 sequent presentation and quotient: relative free completion does not by itself prove an existing pure fragment is fully faithful in that target.
- Van Glabbeek–Hughes proof-net commutation equality: equality on cut-free named formula occurrences; avoid switching to different net or unit conventions.

**Prior-art outcome possibilities:** exact full theorem exists (record and STOP duplicate proof); necessary bridge follows after a finite explicit comparison (prove only that lemma); precise typed counterexample exists (STOP SC for D*); or mixed conservativity remains undetermined (submit a frozen theorem card for constructive/adversarial S3 attempts).
