# Stage 0.7 — Common conjecture contract (PROPOSED FREEZE)

**Status: proposed for owner approval, not an established theorem.** PR #7 and PR #8 are archived as research evidence; neither is a proof or refutation of this statement. **No agent may change this question during a research attempt.** Changes require an explicit versioned owner decision before proof work.

## Why this exists

Previous agents studied *different conjectures* despite instructions to test a common claim. This document chooses one baseline and separates required structure from optional stronger properties. It is not permission to start another unconstrained literature survey.

## Proposed baseline H (faithful rather than full)

Let **C** be the independently defined class of *finite-schematic deductive presentations with binding and finite proof-equation schemas*, as introduced in Report 002 §2.1, subject to the following clarifications: local rule applicability must be effectively checkable; finite proofs form typed compositions; proof equality is the congruence generated by the source's explicitly specified equations. Cyclic or infinitary derivations without finite checkable certificates are excluded. **Membership in C may not depend on the chosen target algebra.** We must audit this definition for implicit inclusion of arbitrary effective proof checkers.

Does there exist a fixed, finitely presented, typed many-sorted algebra A with finite generating operation schemas, fixed typing rules and fixed proof congruence, such that **for every S in C** there is an effective translation of source expressions, judgments, contexts and proofs into A satisfying all clauses below?

1. **Derivability preservation and reflection:** source judgment derivable iff its translated judgment has *some* target proof under the translated assumptions. This is a separate obligation from equality faithfulness.
2. **Proof-class faithfulness:** for source proofs d,e of a common source judgment, d ≈_S e iff F_S(d) ≈_A F_S(e). Equations of S cannot simply be copied into the target's primitive equality.
3. **Composition and substitution:** F_S preserves identities, composition/cut or grafting, capture-avoiding substitution and binding structures when defined in S, modulo the declared equality. State any necessary coherence laws.
4. **Resource integrity:** source constraints on weakening, contraction, ordering and linear splitting must not be bypassed by translating to operations that prove additional translated judgments. Track boundary occurrences and relevant modalities.
5. **Trust separation:** environment may provide mathematical signatures, definitions, explicit assumptions and independently justified theorems; it may **not** supply new trusted inference rules, arbitrary proof-checking oracles, or source-specific equations for target equality. Any certified derived macro must include a derivation from the fixed core and declared assumptions.
6. **Non-vacuity:** source proofs cannot merely be serialized as data and accepted by a universal interpreter. Propose a formal, encoding-invariant (or carefully translation-relative) mathematical criterion excluding such interpreters. This criterion is **currently an open obligation**, not silently solved by invoking D2. D2 is an explicit candidate to test, not adopted as unquestionable truth.
7. **Causal fidelity where defined:** preserve source dependency, independence and conflict structures under explicit typed event maps; do not impose a global total order. If this cannot be formulated uniformly for all C, define a separately motivated subclass C_causal **before** testing and report its effect on H.

**Not required:** fullness/surjectivity on all target proof objects; minimal cardinality of generators; decidable proof equivalence; polynomial proof translation; a universal proof-search procedure; or preservation of source proofs' exact raw chronology.

**Note:** Clauses 1 and 4 can clash with typical embeddings, while 6 and 7 still require mathematically precise quantifiers. Consequently H is an *audit target*, not yet fully formal. If H cannot be made coherent without substantially restricting C, that itself must be reported as a failure of the proposed freeze.

## Prior outcomes and their limits

- PR #7: an informal cardinality obstruction conditional on fullness and finitely many native atomic profiles; a finite group-theoretic faithful-embedding countercheck. Neither decides H.
- PR #8: a first-order modal-logic correspondence result about derivability for a specified modal-logic family. No proof-identity or causal preservation theorem for H.
- PRs #1–#5: strong existing logical frameworks, translation and proof-identity results, alongside unproved claims and corrections. They inform the baseline; they do not establish H.

## Required research gate before another pair of agents

First perform a **definition consistency audit** of H, including a concrete example in C and exact proof-judgment translations. Check whether D2 admits existing generic logical frameworks and whether any universal-checker disguise passes. The owners must then explicitly accept H version 1 (possibly narrower with logged changes) before two agents separately attempt existence and obstruction.

The next two agents must receive **the same immutable file version and commit SHA**. Both must restate H precisely and are forbidden to choose different source classes, interfaces, fullness conditions, or environments. They may propose alternative statements in clearly separate appendices, never as replacements for H.

## Acceptance criteria

A report must provide both the strongest positive and negative attack **against this identical target**; exact theorems with verified hypotheses; any constructed proof labeled as informal unless machine checked; exact missing lemmas; an explicit bridge to the universal objective; and a decision that does not count restricted case studies as universal results.

If non-vacuity or causal fidelity remains undefined, the gate cannot be marked passed. The correct decision is **definition unresolved**, not "existence proved" or "impossibility proved."
